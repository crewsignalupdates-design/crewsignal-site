Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · CrewSignal LLC

CrewSignal publishes independent informational research for aviation professionals.

Subscription information and consent

Our public subscription page submits the information you provide directly to Intuit Mailchimp for newsletter management, including your email address, name, airline/base fields, and separate marketing-permission choices. Mailchimp processes subscriber status, unsubscribe requests, and any confirmation steps required by audience settings. A form submission is not itself proof that a subscription is confirmed.

The regular-newsletter permission and the optional special-notices permission are separate. We do not treat the optional special-notices choice as automatically granted. You may unsubscribe using the link in our emails.

Older CrewSignal forms used Netlify for intake. Those historical submissions are retained separately for review and are not automatically imported into Mailchimp or treated as marketing consent.

Website data and service providers

Our website is hosted on Netlify, which may process technical request data and security logs. Mailchimp processes subscription records and newsletter delivery. Their services may operate in different jurisdictions. For details on their practices, consult the providers' privacy notices.

CrewSignal does not sell subscriber information. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the website, distribute consented updates, and maintain account security.

Contact, retention, and privacy requests

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send. We retain correspondence and operational records for appropriate editorial, security, accounting, and legal recordkeeping purposes, subject to applicable requirements; not all messages are deleted immediately upon resolution.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we control, contact crewsignalupdates@gmail.com. The rights and exceptions that apply depend on your jurisdiction and the nature of the records.

Postal address: CrewSignal LLC, PO Box 136, San Pedro, CA 90733-0136.

Security and policy changes

We use HTTPS and account-security controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; avoid sending sensitive employment, medical, or financial documents through public forms.

We may update this policy when our data practices change, with the effective date shown above.
