Alaska + Hawaiian: The Live Clock

The transaction closed on 2024.09.18, the NMB found a single Flight Attendant transportation system on 2025.03.06, and the JNC opened bargaining on 2025.03.18. At the November 20 measurement date, 612 calendar days had elapsed since the first joint bargaining session. This is an ongoing interval, not a completed time to ratification.

CWA-AFA’s July 2025 estimate that finalizing the JCBA could take two or more years is not a contractual deadline. The sections settled, unresolved economic terms, and implementation milestones will determine the outcome.

Continental + United: The Long Full-JCBA Path

The merger closed on 2010.10.01. The NMB found a single transportation system for Flight Attendants on 2011.04.01. The parties announced a joint agreement on 2016.06.24, and members ratified it on 2016.08.12.

That produced a 2,142-day close-to-ratification interval and a 1,960-day NMB-to-ratification interval. Ratification also did not equal instantaneous operational integration; implementation remained a separate process.

Republic + Mesa: A Limited Bridge Agreement

CWA-AFA and IBT Local 135 entered a merger transition agreement on 2025.10.19. The transaction closed on 2025.11.25. The parties reached a JCBA on 2025.12.11, and Flight Attendants ratified it on 2026.01.22.

The 95-day transition-agreement-to-ratification interval is not directly comparable with a complete Alaska + Hawaiian or Continental + United bargain. The Republic agreement was used as the base, the expedited bargain was limited to specified subjects, and the unions preserved the right to begin full contract negotiations in January 2027.

What Flight Attendants Should Watch

  • Scope of the bargain: A fast limited bridge and a full JCBA are not equivalent achievements.
  • Bargaining cadence: Count sessions, but also measure tentative sections, economic exchanges and unresolved high-value provisions.
  • Ratification versus implementation: A signed agreement matters only if the merged operation can implement scheduling, seniority, pay and work rules promptly and accurately.
  • Bargaining results: Compare the protections retained, terms improved and implementation milestones met.

These intervals have different starting events and different agreement scopes. An ongoing first-session clock cannot be divided by another carrier’s NMB-to-ratification or transition-agreement-to-ratification interval to rank negotiating performance. Compare like milestones where available, and evaluate contract scope and implementation separately.

Methodology and Limits

Elapsed calendar days equal the end date minus the start date, excluding the start date. Ongoing Alaska + Hawaiian measurements end on November 20, 2026; completed comparator intervals end at their stated milestone. Transaction close, single-system determination, certification, first bargaining, ratification, and implementation are distinct events. Different starting events and agreement scopes are not combined into a performance ranking.
