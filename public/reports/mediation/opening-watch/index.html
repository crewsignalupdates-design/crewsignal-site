When a Watch Becomes an Active Tracker

An active tracker requires a documented opening milestone. The examples below distinguish an announced formal opening from member preparation:

  • a Section 6 or equivalent formal opening notice;
  • an exchange of opening proposals;
  • an official announcement that direct bargaining began;
  • a documented first bargaining session; or
  • an NMB mediation application or docket.

Surveys, committee training, suggestion boxes and approaching amendable dates are meaningful preparation, but they do not by themselves establish that formal negotiations have begun.

JetBlue / TWU Local 579

JetBlue’s Inflight agreement becomes amendable December 13, 2026. A signed LOA provides an early-opening right, but a right to open is not evidence that negotiations have begun.

JetBlue remains on Opening Watch pending a documented formal opening notice, exchanged proposals, an official announcement that direct bargaining began, a first bargaining session, or an NMB mediation filing.

What to watch: a dated Flight Attendant opening notice, exchanged proposals, or a confirmed first session.

Endeavor / CWA-AFA

Endeavor’s CWA-AFA Master Executive Council reported on October 8 that its negotiations survey was complete and that members’ responses would help prepare the contract opener.

In its November 17 committee report, CWA-AFA said its first bargaining sessions with Endeavor were scheduled for January 25–26, 2027. The committee was still finalizing opening proposals. Scheduling the first meetings does not establish that bargaining has begun, so Endeavor remains on Opening Watch.

Republic + Mesa / IBT — Full Successor Bargaining Watch

The January 22, 2026 joint agreement delivered limited midterm improvements while preserving the right to full negotiations beginning in January 2027.

The NMB’s October 13 representation determination certified IBT for the combined Flight Attendant group; it did not open successor bargaining. The full successor round remains on Opening Watch.

This was a certification determination, not an election-result announcement or a new collective bargaining agreement. The decision does not identify an administering IBT local.

CrewSignal Assessment

Opening Watch prevents two opposite errors: declaring bargaining active before the public record supports it, and ignoring the preparation period that shapes bargaining priorities and member expectations. The same threshold will be applied regardless of union.
