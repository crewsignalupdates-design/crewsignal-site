CrewSignal Assessment

PSA’s elapsed time is one of the clearest examples of why amendable dates should be visible. Shared legal support, international leadership, NMB-supervised sessions and a defined calendar demonstrate multi-carrier resources in use; the outcome test remains whether TA2 produces ratifiable economics and timely implementation.

Because the 1,133-day measure includes both TA1 and TA2, it should be used to describe the workgroup’s total wait beyond the contractual benchmark—not to imply that the present TA2 committee controlled the entire period. No new written contractual result was verified this week.
