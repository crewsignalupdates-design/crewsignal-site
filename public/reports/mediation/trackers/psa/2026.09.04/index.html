CrewSignal Assessment

The September 4 date is evidence of an active mediated process, but scheduling a session is an input. At 1,147 days beyond the benchmark, the performance measure is whether TA2 produces a ratifiable economic package materially better than the earlier outcome.

A prompt written recap would let members distinguish productive movement from another calendar entry. CrewSignal will not treat the absence of an immediate report as proof that nothing happened, but the union’s advocacy for institutional scale is stronger when resource deployment is matched by transparent, measurable results.
