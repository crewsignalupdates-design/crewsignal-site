Process Signals

  • The August 4 update remains the latest detailed public bargaining account: CWA-AFA presented a targeted proposal seeking a larger raise, boarding pay upon ratification and a larger signing-bonus pool.   • The NMB weekly activity report for August 10–14 did not identify a PSA-specific docket, reassignment, ratification or closure. No new tentative agreement, ratification vote, release from mediation or cooling-off period was publicly announced during the reporting window.

CrewSignal Assessment

PSA offers concrete evidence of multi-carrier resources being deployed: professional legal support, international leadership participation, NMB-supervised bargaining and a defined follow-up calendar. Those inputs may strengthen leverage and member understanding, but the outcome test remains the written TA2 economics and whether Flight Attendants receive enough verified improvement to ratify the agreement. This week added member engagement, not a new contractual result.
