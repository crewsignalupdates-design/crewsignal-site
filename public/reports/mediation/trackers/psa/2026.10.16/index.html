Negotiations & Mediation

PSA / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.16

Mediated bargaining for a revised tentative agreement

Status This Week

CWA-AFA’s October 13 PSA bulletin reported an additional $20 in daily per diem for crew members with VPS overnights on October 12, 13, and 14 following storm disruption. The temporary arrangement is separate from negotiations for a revised comprehensive agreement.

Process Signals

  • The union attributed the additional payment to discussions with management about disrupted hotel and food arrangements. Its notice applies to the specified VPS overnights, not a systemwide increase in contractual per-diem rates.
  • The announcement reports an agreement on the temporary payment; it does not verify that the money had already appeared in every affected crew member’s pay.
  • The union’s earlier August recap described the July 31 NMB-overseen bargaining session. Its October 9 regional-bargaining announcement continued to identify PSA as seeking a new contract; neither source announced a full replacement settlement.

CrewSignal Assessment

The VPS payment is a specific result reported for affected crews. It should be assessed on those terms, without treating it as a full tentative agreement or as evidence that the wider economic dispute is resolved.
