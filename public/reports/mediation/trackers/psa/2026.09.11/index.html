Week ending 2026.09.11

Status This Week

No written PSA TA2 recap of the September 4 virtual NMB-mediated session was located by the 2026.09.11 cutoff. The public TA2 page continued to list the August 4 proposal account and August 5 scheduling notice as its newest updates. The next announced mediation date was September 22 in person.

Contract timing: The published PSA agreement was effective through 2023.07.15. The available public source does not label that date an amendable date, so CrewSignal treats it as a contractual benchmark rather than a formally verified amendable date. As of 2026.09.11, 3 years, 1 month and 27 days—1,154 days—had elapsed since that benchmark. That full clock spans the original process, TA1 and TA2; it is not attributable solely to the current committee.

Process Signals

  • The NMB FY2027 budget justification continues to identify PSA among CWA-AFA Flight Attendant mediation cases. At CrewSignal’s source check, the NMB weekly index had not posted a report for September 7–11.
  • The latest detailed public proposal remained CWA-AFA’s request for a larger raise, boarding pay on ratification and a larger signing-bonus pool. The approximately 30 percent immediate-wage figure remains the union’s estimate of its proposal, not a negotiated commitment.
  • A staff attorney and international leadership have been visibly deployed, and additional dates were secured. The missing September 4 recap means those institutional inputs cannot yet be connected to a publicly verified proposal movement or tentative agreement.

CrewSignal Assessment

At 1,154 days beyond the contractual benchmark, a one-week reporting lag after a mediated session is more than a communications detail: it prevents members from evaluating whether the accelerated TA2 strategy is working.

CrewSignal does not infer that the September 4 session was unproductive. It does conclude that multi-carrier resources should be judged through timely disclosure and a ratifiable economic result, not merely by the presence of staff, leadership and scheduled dates.
