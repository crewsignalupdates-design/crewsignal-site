Negotiations & Mediation

PSA / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.09

Status This Week

On October 5, PSA CWA-AFA challenged management’s announced change to continuous medical-leave eligibility, scheduled to take effect October 12. The union said the qualifying absence would increase from seven to fourteen days and that its legal department was reviewing the policy.

Process Signals

  • The medical-leave dispute is a same-week representational development, not an announced bargaining settlement. The union’s concerns about the contract, past practice, and applicable law were under review; the bulletin did not report a legal ruling.
  • The September 23 bargaining recap described the preceding day’s mediated session. Management added $100,000 to its proposed signing-bonus pool but did not increase its proposed wage rates, according to CWA-AFA.
  • That recap also identified disagreement over scheduled-days-off provisions and listed further mediated meetings for October 23, November 2, 13, and 20, and December 7. Those future dates were not completed sessions as of October 9.

CrewSignal Assessment

PSA’s record combines an immediate leave-policy dispute with unresolved contract economics and scheduling protections. Neither the proposed signing payment nor the scheduled mediation dates establishes a complete revised agreement. The October 5 policy challenge should be followed separately from any later negotiated resolution.
