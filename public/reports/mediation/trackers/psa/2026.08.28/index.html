CrewSignal Assessment

The 1,140-day post-benchmark period remains one of the strongest reasons to distinguish institutional inputs from outcomes. NMB oversight, staff counsel, international leadership, town halls and scheduled sessions demonstrate resource deployment; members still need a ratifiable agreement that improves the economics rejected or found inadequate in the earlier process.

Because the timing measure includes TA1 and TA2, it describes the workgroup’s total wait beyond the contractual benchmark, not the tenure or responsibility of the present TA2 committee alone. The absence of an August 19 written recap is a transparency gap, but it does not establish that no bargaining movement occurred.
