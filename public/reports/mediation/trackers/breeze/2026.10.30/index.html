Breeze / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Week ending 2026.10.30

First-contract negotiations · No prior-contract amendability date

Status This Week

CWA-AFA reported that its committee met Breeze management October 19–21 and worked on Hours of Service, Scheduling, Sick Leave and Part Time provisions. The union said the parties made substantial progress on sick-leave and absence issues, while economic accrual questions and the reliability policy remained open.

Process Signals

  • The committee described Hours of Service and Scheduling as advanced but still unresolved. Those sections govern core work rules including bidding, trading, reassignments, days off and duty limits.
  • Part-time provisions remained in an earlier stage of discussion. CWA-AFA said it was seeking protections for existing part-time employees, more full-time opportunities and improvements for both groups.
  • The next bargaining session was scheduled for November 17–19. The October update did not announce a complete tentative agreement or a ratification timetable.

CrewSignal Assessment

The October session shows movement on several central first-contract subjects, especially sick leave and scheduling. Economic terms and other unresolved provisions still separate section-level progress from a complete agreement.
