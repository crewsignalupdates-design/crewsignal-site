CrewSignal Assessment

CWA-AFA’s comparative contract library is a plausible advantage, and the July account shows it being used. At 856 days since certification, the more important question is whether that expertise is accelerating a complete first agreement rather than producing an open-ended drafting process.

Transparency is part of representation quality. A multi-carrier union asking members to value its scale should publish enough current information to show section movement, management delay and the concrete contribution of shared staff.
