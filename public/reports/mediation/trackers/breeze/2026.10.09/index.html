First-contract negotiations · No existing contract amendable date

Status This Week

Breeze remains a first-contract negotiations tracker. No dated Flight Attendant bargaining announcement establishing a complete tentative agreement, ratification timetable, or move into federal mediation was identified for October 3–9.

Process Signals

  • The August 23 CWA-AFA update reported bargaining during the week of August 17. The union presented a proposal addressing part-time Flight Attendants, and management delivered its first Scheduling counterproposal.
  • The update described the remaining sections as complex and said the committee would develop its response. That is evidence of continued proposal exchanges, not agreed scheduling language.
  • CWA-AFA’s February 17, 2025 bulletin identifies February 11, 2025 as the start of contract negotiations. That bargaining start is distinct from union certification and is not an amendable date.

CrewSignal Assessment

The documented record supports continued first-contract bargaining with important work-rule issues outstanding. It does not support describing a deal as imminent or treating another employee group’s mediation proceeding as a Flight Attendant development.
