Breeze / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Week ending 2026.10.16

First-contract negotiations · No existing contract amendable date

Status This Week

Breeze Flight Attendants remained in first-contract negotiations. No new dated bargaining outcome, complete tentative agreement, or ratification timetable was verified in the public sources reviewed for October 10–16.

Process Signals

  • The union’s August bargaining update described a part-time proposal from CWA-AFA and management’s first Scheduling counterproposal. Those exchanges were proposals, not agreed contract language.
  • The NMB certified CWA-AFA to represent Breeze Flight Attendants on May 8, 2024. Representation certification establishes the bargaining representative; it is not a mediation docket or an agreement on contract terms.
  • No dated source reviewed for this reporting week established a move into federal mediation. First-contract bargaining has no prior Flight Attendant agreement from which to calculate a post-amendability clock.

CrewSignal Assessment

Scheduling and the other terms of a first agreement remain the central bargaining questions. The public record does not support predicting an imminent settlement, and the absence of a new dated recap does not establish that talks were inactive.
