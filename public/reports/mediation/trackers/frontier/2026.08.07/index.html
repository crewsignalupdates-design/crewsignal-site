Negotiations & Mediation

Frontier / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.08.07

Status This Week

On 2026.08.07, CWA-AFA announced a Frontier Flight Attendant picket for 2026.09.08 at the carrier’s headquarters. The union also said Frontier recently withdrew four previously agreed provisions and had not supplied a counterproposal on compensation; those descriptions are the union’s account of bargaining.

Process Signals

  • Frontier’s public CWA-AFA status chart lists the company as owing responses on compensation, hours of service, scheduling and reserve. The chart shows ten sections tentatively agreed, nine in play, seven still to be proposed and one deadlocked.
  • The National Mediation Board’s FY2027 budget justification continues to list Frontier among the CWA-AFA flight attendant cases in federal mediation.
  • The September picket is an escalation in public pressure, not a statutory release from mediation. CrewSignal located no NMB proffer, release or cooling-off-period announcement for Frontier during this reporting window.

CrewSignal Assessment

This is the week’s clearest advocacy escalation among the long-running mediation files. A multi-carrier union can provide communications reach, professional staff and cross-airline mobilization capacity, but the event itself does not move the Railway Labor Act clock toward a strike. The immediate question is whether pressure produces written movement on compensation and preserves, restores or replaces the four provisions the union says management withdrew.
