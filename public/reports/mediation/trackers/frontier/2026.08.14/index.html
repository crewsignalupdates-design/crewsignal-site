CrewSignal Assessment

A multi-carrier union can supply professional bargaining staff, national communications and cross-carrier mobilization for a prolonged mediation campaign. Frontier demonstrates that capacity, but the week also exposes an accountability gap: a scheduled session without a public recap cannot be scored as progress. The next meaningful evidence is a written compensation response, restoration or replacement of disputed prior understandings, additional tentative agreements, or a formal NMB process change.
