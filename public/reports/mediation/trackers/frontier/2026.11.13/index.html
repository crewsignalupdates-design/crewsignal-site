Status This Week

No dated public recap announced a new Frontier bargaining outcome during the week, and no complete tentative agreement or ratification timetable was announced. The November 17 Atlanta picket was scheduled for the following week.

Process Signals

  • Frontier’s October 28 Form 10-Q states that Flight Attendant negotiations were in National Mediation Board (NMB) mediation, with monthly meetings. This is earlier management-reported context, not confirmation of November session results.
  • CWA’s November 5 newsletter reported that CWA-AFA members planned another Atlanta picket for November 17. Mobilization is distinct from agreement at the bargaining table.
  • The announced picket does not authorize a strike. Under the Railway Labor Act, self-help becomes available only after NMB release and the statutory 30-day cooling-off period, subject to possible Presidential Emergency Board proceedings.

CrewSignal Assessment

The available evidence supports continued mediation as the last verified posture, without a newly documented bargaining milestone. A session recap, complete tentative agreement or formal NMB action would establish a change. Limited public reporting does not prove that talks were inactive.
