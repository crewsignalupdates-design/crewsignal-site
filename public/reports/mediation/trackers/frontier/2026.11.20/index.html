Negotiations & Mediation

Frontier / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.11.20

Mediated successor-contract negotiations

Status This Week

Frontier Flight Attendants and supporters picketed outside Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport on November 17. CWA-AFA’s November 20 recap called for a new contract without further delay. At the rally, a union representative said members wanted a living wage and raised compensation for time on the job, including airport delays.

Process Signals

  • Frontier told CBS Atlanta it remained committed to bargaining through the National Mediation Board (NMB). In its October Form 10-Q, the company reported that its first Flight Attendant mediation meeting was in February 2025 and that sessions were taking place monthly.
  • The informational picket did not establish a change in proposals, a complete tentative agreement, a ratification timetable or an NMB release.
  • Release from mediation and the ensuing 30-day cooling-off period are separate from picketing. An informational picket does not itself authorize self-help.

CrewSignal Assessment

The Atlanta action made pay and compensation for time on duty more visible. The last documented bargaining stage remained federal mediation; the public record for the week did not show a settlement or the start of a cooling-off period.
