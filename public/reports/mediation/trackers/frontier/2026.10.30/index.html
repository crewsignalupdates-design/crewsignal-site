Frontier / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.30

Amendable 2024.05.15 · 898 elapsed calendar days as of 2026.10.30

Status This Week

CWA-AFA’s October 30 recap confirms that Frontier Flight Attendants picketed at Philadelphia International Airport on October 26. The union reported support from other Flight Attendant groups and labor organizations while continuing its campaign for a successor agreement.

Process Signals

  • In an October 26 statement reported by 6abc, Frontier said the parties remained in NMB-supervised mediation and were meeting monthly. The airline said it sought an agreement that addressed bargaining issues while supporting its long-term viability.
  • CWA-AFA’s October 30 recap announced an Atlanta picket for November 17. At the October 30 cutoff, that was a planned event rather than a completed action.
  • Picketing does not itself authorize a strike. Release from mediation and the statutory cooling-off process are separate NMB steps.
  • The May 15, 2024 amendability date gives 898 elapsed calendar days through October 30. This is not a count of bargaining days or time in mediation.

CrewSignal Assessment

The Philadelphia action increased visible public pressure, but neither the union recap nor the company statement announced revised contract terms or a complete tentative agreement. Bargaining and public mobilization therefore remained separate tracks.
