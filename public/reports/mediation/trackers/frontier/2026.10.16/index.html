Frontier / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.16

Amendable 2024.05.15 · 884 elapsed calendar days as of 2026.10.16

Status This Week

Frontier’s mediated successor-contract negotiations remained unresolved in the dated public sources reviewed for October 10–16. No complete tentative agreement, ratification result, or release from mediation was verified during that window.

Process Signals

  • Frontier’s 2025 Form 10-K states that CWA-AFA requested mediation in October 2024 and that mediated meetings began in February 2025.
  • An October 9 CWA-AFA announcement scheduled Philadelphia picketing for October 26. At the October 16 cutoff, this was a planned activity, not a completed event or a bargaining agreement.
  • CWA’s September account of headquarters picketing attributed the bargaining dispute in part to management’s rejection of the union’s boarding-pay proposal. That remains the union’s account of an earlier dispute.
  • CWA-AFA identifies May 15, 2024 as the agreement’s amendable date. The 884-day figure measures elapsed calendar time through October 16.

CrewSignal Assessment

Member mobilization and progress at the bargaining table are related but distinct. The announced picket demonstrates a planned pressure campaign; it does not establish new contract terms or a change in the NMB process. Limited public reporting should not be treated as evidence that no negotiations occurred.
