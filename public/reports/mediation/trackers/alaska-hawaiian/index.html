Current Status

Alaska Air Group completed its acquisition of Hawaiian Airlines on 2024.09.18. The National Mediation Board found a single transportation system for the Flight Attendant craft or class on 2025.03.06 and certified CWA-AFA as representative on 2025.04.11.

The Joint Negotiating Committee opened JCBA bargaining on 2025.03.18. Its Session 19 update, published September 14, reports September 8–10 meetings in Seattle and exchanges on Grievance, Training, Performance Based Pay, and Retirement.

That update scheduled NMB-led interest-based bargaining training for October 14–15 and the next bargaining session for October 26–28. A scheduled training event is not confirmation that it occurred or that the case entered statutory mediation.

The October 6 Reserve survey announcement sets a closing time of noon Pacific on October 20. Survey responses inform a proposal; they are not agreed contract language.

Process Signals

  • This is a full merger JCBA combining separate pre-merger agreements, not ordinary successor bargaining over one existing contract.
  • Until a JCBA is ratified and implemented, the pre-merger groups continue under separate agreements, work rules, seniority lists and scheduling systems.
  • Session count is useful only when paired with substance: sections exchanged, tentative agreements reached, economic issues opened and implementation planning completed.

CrewSignal Assessment

For United Flight Attendants, Alaska + Hawaiian is a live test of whether a same-union merger can produce a materially faster and more transparent JCBA than the Continental + United integration did. The comparison must remain disciplined: speed matters, but so do the breadth of the bargain, preservation of stronger provisions, seniority implementation and the time between ratification and actual operational integration.

CrewSignal will therefore track three clocks separately: transaction close, NMB single-system determination and first joint bargaining session.
