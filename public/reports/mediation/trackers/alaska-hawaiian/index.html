Current Status

Alaska Air Group completed its acquisition of Hawaiian Airlines on 2024.09.18. The National Mediation Board found a single transportation system for the Flight Attendant craft or class on 2025.03.06 and certified CWA-AFA as representative on 2025.04.11.

JCBA bargaining began on 2025.03.18. In its October 30 Session 20 recap, CWA-AFA reported meetings in Seattle on October 26–28 and tentative agreements on Seniority, legacy retiree pass privileges and retiree health insurance. These are section-level agreements, not a completed JCBA.

The committee also discussed Grievance Procedures, Performance Based Pay and Retirement, and Training. It confirmed that NMB-led interest-based bargaining training occurred October 14–15 and said the parties planned to combine that approach with traditional bargaining. Training does not itself establish statutory mediation.

The committee reported more than 1,000 Reserve-survey responses and scheduled additional review for November 6–9. The next management bargaining session was scheduled for November 16–18. Through the November 6 reporting cutoff, those schedules did not establish completed meetings or additional tentative agreements.

Separate pre-merger Hawaiian aircraft negotiations: On November 6, CWA-AFA reported that it had received official notice on September 8 of plans to acquire 787-10 aircraft. A negotiating committee was scheduled to meet management November 19–20 under the Hawaiian agreement’s new-aircraft provision. This is separate from the full Alaska + Hawaiian JCBA and does not establish completed aircraft terms or a new JCBA session.

Process Signals

  • This is a full merger JCBA combining separate pre-merger agreements, not ordinary successor bargaining over one existing contract.
  • Until a JCBA is ratified and implemented, the pre-merger groups continue under separate agreements, work rules, seniority lists and scheduling systems.
  • Session count is useful only when paired with substance: sections exchanged, tentative agreements reached, economic issues opened and implementation planning completed.

CrewSignal Assessment

Alaska + Hawaiian is negotiating a full joint agreement from two established contracts represented by CWA-AFA. Progress can be assessed through the sections settled, unresolved terms, protections retained, and implementation milestones. A shorter elapsed interval alone does not establish a stronger agreement.

Transaction close, the NMB single-system finding, and the first joint bargaining session remain separately labeled milestones. The elapsed-calendar-day measurements shown here do not predict completion.
