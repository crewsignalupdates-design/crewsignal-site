Agreement and Implementation References

The official final 2026–2031 CBA is the current base agreement. It became effective May 31, 2026, while LOA 9 and dated Joint Implementation Team notices govern staged implementation. A provision’s appearance in the agreement does not, by itself, establish that it is operational.

Implementation Updates

CWA-AFA’s November 11 Joint Implementation Team update says Section 29.A.2.g. took effect with the October 2026 bid month and explains the 480-hour paid-activity measurement used to determine eligibility for employer-subsidized healthcare for the 2028 plan year. The notice also states that provisions not yet announced as implemented remain out of effect.

Earlier October guidance explained commuter-program provisions implemented on October 16. These notices document specific implementation steps; they do not establish that every provision of the agreement is operational.
