CrewSignal Assessment

The elapsed period is now a core performance measure. A multi-carrier union can bring staff, research, legal support and institutional continuity to a small carrier, but the public outcome record still must show what that capacity has produced. This week’s sources establish no new tentative agreement, comprehensive settlement, ratification schedule or formal NMB status change.

The public sources do not allocate responsibility for the full delay among management positions, union strategy, mediator availability or session scheduling. CrewSignal therefore treats the duration as a documented accountability fact—not proof that one party caused every day of delay.
