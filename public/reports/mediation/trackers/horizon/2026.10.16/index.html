Horizon Air / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.16

Mediated successor-contract negotiations

Status This Week

In its October 11 recap, CWA-AFA reported continuing economic disagreements after the October 5–6 mediated session in Chicago and announced a request for an NMB status conference. Flight Attendants also picketed on October 14 at six airports, according to the union’s October 16 report.

Process Signals

  • The recap described management proposals affecting minimum duty-period credit, the lineholder guarantee, and pay for the most senior Flight Attendants. CWA-AFA sought broader wage and benefit improvements. Those positions were proposals, not an agreed economic package.
  • The union described the parties as at an impasse but expressly said its status-conference request did not mean the NMB had declared one. The recap did not announce a conference date or release from mediation.
  • The October 16 picketing account confirms that the October 14 activity occurred. Picketing demonstrates member mobilization; it is separate from a contract settlement.

CrewSignal Assessment

The conference request seeks further Board attention to the unresolved economic issues. It does not start a cooling-off period or authorize a strike. The next procedural development would be a confirmed NMB response or another bargaining result, not an assumption that mediation has ended.
