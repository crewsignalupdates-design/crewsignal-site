Financial Data Linkage

Explore CWA-AFA’s financial reports and the Horizon Air filing directory for available union receipts, expenditures, and transfers between affiliates. The master report explains how CWA, the CWA-AFA sector, and Master and Local Executive Council (MEC and LEC) filings fit together. These records do not provide a complete allocation of every carrier’s dues or representational costs.

Financial-data qualification: The linked master and Horizon reports currently disagree on FY2023 MEC funding and the resulting local-council percentage. Those figures are not reconciled and should not be used as settled amounts or representation-value measures.
