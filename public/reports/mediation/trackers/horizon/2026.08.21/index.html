CrewSignal Assessment

Horizon’s published calendar provides useful transparency, but the duration is now significant: more than two years beyond the amendable date and more than one year in mediation. The next analytical test is whether the August session yields documented proposal movement or additional tentative agreements.

Multi-carrier legal and bargaining support can strengthen the committee, while a strike-authorization vote can increase internal solidarity and bargaining pressure. Neither resource deployment nor the vote is itself a contract result, and the vote is not a direct strike trigger under the RLA.
