Piedmont / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Week ending 2026.11.20

Direct successor-contract negotiations

Status This Week

On November 17, CWA-AFA Piedmont Council 61 invited Flight Attendants to a December 9 Regional Day of Action, including events in Charlotte and Philadelphia. It was a call for member participation, not an announcement of new contractual terms or a ratification vote.

Process Signals

  • Council 61’s calendar lists November 19 as union negotiating-committee preparation, not a bargaining session with Piedmont.
  • A separate November 9 notice scheduled a November 16 meeting with Crew Scheduling Management about how scheduling requests are handled. The union described that as a scheduling-process discussion, not successor-contract bargaining.
  • The November 17 union notice dated the start of the current contract talks to November 17, 2025.

CrewSignal Assessment

The public record still points to direct successor-contract bargaining. The planned action and committee preparation did not establish that new proposals were exchanged, that the parties reached a new section agreement, or that bargaining moved to federal mediation.
