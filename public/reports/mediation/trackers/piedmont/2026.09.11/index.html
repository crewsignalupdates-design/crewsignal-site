Status This Week

No written Piedmont bargaining update dated during the 2026.09.05–2026.09.11 reporting window was located. The public newsletter archive continued to show August 12 briefing notices as its newest negotiations-related entries.

Process Signals

  • The last detailed written update located reported fifteen tentatively agreed sections. CrewSignal continues that count rather than infer a change from member briefings or an undocumented session.
  • The standing negotiations page still displayed an older June session notice at the source check, while the newsletter archive showed no September bargaining entry. That limits the public’s ability to track section responsibility and progress.
  • Piedmont remains publicly classified as direct negotiations rather than one of the continuing CWA-AFA mediation matters identified in the NMB FY2027 budget justification.

CrewSignal Assessment

The 193-day post-marker period is comparatively short and the reported fifteen tentative agreements indicate meaningful early progress. The lack of a durable August or September recap is nevertheless an accountability weakness because members cannot independently determine whether momentum continued.

CWA-AFA’s multi-carrier model should be able to provide both technical contract expertise and consistent public reporting. The Piedmont record presently supports the first claim more clearly than the second.
