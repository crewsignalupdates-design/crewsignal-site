Piedmont / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Successor-contract negotiations · Exact amendability day not established

The background below covers sources through March 20, 2026. Weekly Updates and Monthly Archives contain later dated reporting.

This historical overview distinguishes the agreement’s term, reported bargaining activity, and the type of NMB proceeding. Union communications describe bargaining positions; official docket entries establish only the proceeding they identify.

Key Takeaways

  • The published agreement states a term from March 2, 2022 through March 2, 2026 and contains renewal and notice provisions. The cover dates alone are not used here to assign an exact amendability day.
  • American’s 2025 Form 10-K lists Piedmont CWA-AFA Flight Attendants as a represented group with a 2026 amendable date.
  • CrewSignal did not identify a public Flight Attendant contract-mediation docket for Piedmont through 2026.03.20.
  • The only NMB item CrewSignal located naming Piedmont CWA-AFA in FY2025 was GM-064, and the NMB’s annual report defines “GM” as Grievance Mediation rather than a collective-bargaining mediation docket.
  • The union’s November 2025 opening proposal and its later dated updates describe the demands and activity in successor bargaining. A proposal is not an agreed benefit.

Background & Context

Piedmont’s agreement and the union’s opening proposal describe different milestones: the existing contract’s term and the proposals for its successor. Bargaining may begin before a contract’s amendable date.

The historical overview ends March 20, 2026. Subsequent proposals, section agreements, and sessions appear in the dated reports.

Amendable Date and Immediate Posture

The agreement cover states a March 2, 2022–March 2, 2026 term. Its renewal and notice language also matters; this page does not infer March 3 as the exact amendability day from the cover alone.

American’s 2025 Form 10-K lists Piedmont CWA-AFA Flight Attendants as a represented group with a 2026 amendable date. The filing does not establish the exact day or the date of the first bargaining meeting.

What the Public Record Shows About Bargaining

CWA-AFA Piedmont’s November 17, 2025 opening proposal seeks changes to pay, boarding pay, reserve, sit time, insurance, retirement, and days off, while retaining Recognition & Scope as current language. These are proposed terms, not a completed agreement.

CWA-AFA Piedmont’s public negotiations page also shows that bargaining sessions were already underway before the amendable date and remained active near the start of the amendable window. As of the page snapshot CrewSignal reviewed, the site listed “Negotiations Update—Session 3! 12 Sections TA’d! (2/15)” along with late-January and early-February negotiation updates.

Those union publications describe bargaining activity before the historical overview’s March 20, 2026 cutoff. They do not, by themselves, establish how close the parties were to a complete agreement.

Why CrewSignal Treats This as Negotiations, Not Mediation

The public NMB record is the main reason. In the Board’s FY2025 annual report, the list of airline mediation cases docketed includes Piedmont Airlines CWA Passenger Service Employees, but it does not list Piedmont CWA-AFA Flight Attendants.

The only FY2025 NMB item CrewSignal located that names Piedmont CWA-AFA Flight Attendants is GM-064 in the April 21–25, 2025 weekly report. But the NMB’s FY2025 annual report defines “GM” as Grievance Mediation. That means GM-064 should not be read as proof that the Flight Attendant contract negotiations were in formal collective-bargaining mediation.

The cited historical records distinguish direct contract negotiations from grievance mediation. GM-064 is not evidence that the Flight Attendant successor-contract talks entered statutory bargaining mediation.

Scope of the Historical Record

The agreement, company labor disclosures, and attributed union bulletins document different aspects of the bargaining record. Their coverage is not a complete diary of negotiations. The weekly reports distinguish later developments from the historical material on this page.
