Piedmont / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Week ending 2026.10.16

Successor-contract negotiations

Status This Week

Piedmont remained in successor-contract negotiations. No dated October 10–16 announcement of a complete tentative agreement, ratification result, or move into federal mediation was located in the union publications reviewed.

Process Signals

  • The November 17, 2025 union opening proposal sought higher pay, boarding pay, duty-pay protections, retroactive pay, and other compensation improvements. These are bargaining requests, not benefits already secured.
  • CWA-AFA’s October 9 regional-bargaining announcement identified Piedmont among the carriers that had begun negotiations; it did not announce a settlement.
  • The published agreement states a term through March 2, 2026 and includes renewal and notice provisions. The stated term alone does not resolve the exact amendability day.

CrewSignal Assessment

The union’s opening priorities establish what it is seeking, not how much has been agreed. A dated session recap or tentative section language would make the progress of this round clearer. The absence of a new public recap does not establish that no talks occurred.
