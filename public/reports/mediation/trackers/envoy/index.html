Successor-contract negotiations · Amendable 2026.04.01

Weekly Updates and Monthly Archives track subsequent developments.

CWA-AFA reported that Envoy Flight Attendant successor-contract talks were underway in Dallas on March 25–26, 2026. The agreement became amendable on April 1.

Key Takeaways

  • Envoy’s current Flight Attendant agreement reached its amendable point on 2026.04.01.
  • The first union-reported session took place before the amendable date.

Background & Context

Envoy’s existing contract, bargaining announcements, and any NMB proceeding describe separate milestones. A date printed in the agreement should not be substituted for evidence of a meeting or a change in the bargaining process.

Amendable Date and Immediate Posture

The current Envoy Flight Attendant agreement provides the clearest starting point for this round. The agreement’s term runs through 2026.03.31, making 2026.04.01 the first amendable day.

April 1, 2026 is the agreement’s amendable date, not proof of when negotiations opened. Bargaining sessions and later procedural changes are identified separately in the dated updates.

What the Public Record Shows

This landing page preserves the agreement’s duration information and links to the dated bargaining reports. Those reports distinguish announced sessions from completed sessions and proposals from tentative agreements.

Negotiations and Mediation

Direct negotiations and federal mediation are distinct stages. A documented mediation request or docket would support a change in classification; the arrival of an amendable date alone does not.
