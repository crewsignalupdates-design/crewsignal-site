Envoy / CWA-AFA Negotiations

Week ending 2026.08.07

Status This Week

AFA Envoy reported on 2026.08.07 that negotiations continued during the week in Dallas, with the reserve section serving as the principal subject of discussion.

Process Signals

  • The same-week public update confirms table activity but, in the indexed material reviewed by CrewSignal, did not identify a newly completed tentative agreement.
  • The prior detailed update reported that the parties had developed a framework for a long-call reserve system with a twelve-hour callout and still needed to convert the framework into contract language. Continued reserve work is therefore a substantive continuation rather than a new bargaining subject.
  • The official Envoy negotiations page now lists sessions for 2026.08.25–2026.08.27, with a member Zoom on 2026.08.26, and another session for 2026.09.15–2026.09.17.

CrewSignal Assessment

Confirmed table activity and scheduled follow-up dates are stronger process signals than general mobilization alone. The record also shows carrier representatives working with an AFA senior staff negotiator and staff attorney, a concrete example of multi-carrier institutional resources being applied locally. The outcome measure remains completed contract language: reserve discussions have advanced beyond concepts, but no public tentative agreement was identified this week.
