Status This Week

CWA-AFA’s November 8 Update 8 supplies a new public account of the October bargaining session in Dallas. It reports agreement on most Reserve language, but not a completed Reserve section or a complete contract. The exact October meeting dates are not stated in the update.

Process Signals

  • Two Reserve issues remained open: airport-standby duration and assignments during the 45 minutes after standby. Although the narrative describes substantial agreement, its completed tentative-agreement list says none.
  • The union reported Hours of Service proposals seeking more rest on domestic and international trips and additional monthly days off. It was still awaiting the company’s Scheduling proposal when it issued the update.
  • The November 8 update scheduled November bargaining for the week of November 9. No dated recap establishing those meetings’ results was identified. A scheduled November 11 member call is not itself a bargaining outcome.
  • Section 36 supports April 1, 2026 amendability. The 226-day measure is elapsed calendar time, not negotiating days.

CrewSignal Assessment

The November 8 publication clarifies the October discussion rather than proving a November settlement. Reserve and scheduling remain material unresolved subjects in that account. A later session recap is needed to establish what changed in November; the absence of a public recap does not mean bargaining stopped.
