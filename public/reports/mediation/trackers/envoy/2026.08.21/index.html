CrewSignal Assessment

Envoy is comparatively early in its post-amendable period. The relevant near-term test is whether the reserve framework becomes enforceable language and whether scheduled sessions produce section-level tentative agreements. A conceptual framework is movement, but it is not a contractual result.

The presence of senior bargaining and legal staff demonstrates multi-carrier resource deployment. At 142 days past the amendable benchmark, the stronger assessment is to monitor conversion and pace rather than equate Envoy with cases that have remained unresolved for years.
