Status This Week

The published calendar placed Envoy negotiations during the week of 2026.08.24, and CWA-AFA distributed the link for an August 26 member Zoom on August 25. No written post-session bargaining recap dated by the end of the reporting window was located, so CrewSignal records the scheduled activity and member-briefing notice without assigning an unverified result.

Contract timing: The published Envoy agreement runs through 2026.03.31, making 2026.04.01 the working amendable-date benchmark. As of 2026.08.28, 4 months and 27 days—149 days—had elapsed since that date. This is an amendable-date measure; it is not a claim that bargaining began on April 1.

Process Signals

  • The August 7 corrected calendar listed negotiations during the weeks of August 24, September 14, October 19, November 9 and November 30, with an August 26 Zoom described as the venue for real-time discussion with the full negotiating committee.
  • The August 25 newsletter supplied the Zoom link but did not contain a proposal-by-proposal bargaining report. CrewSignal therefore does not convert the existence of the Zoom notice into evidence of section-level progress.
  • Envoy is not listed among the continuing CWA-AFA Flight Attendant mediation cases in the NMB FY2027 budget justification. The defensible public classification remains direct negotiations, and the NMB weekly activity report did not announce a covered-carrier process change.

CrewSignal Assessment

Envoy remains comparatively early in its post-amendable period, and the scheduled week plus member-Zoom notice are evidence of an active process. They are not evidence of contractual movement by themselves. The near-term measure remains conversion of the previously discussed Reserve framework into enforceable language and section-level tentative agreements.

A live briefing can improve member access, but it is not a durable public bargaining record. CWA-AFA’s multi-carrier legal and negotiating support is most persuasive when paired with timely written accounts that show what was proposed, what changed, what was tentatively agreed and what remains unresolved.
