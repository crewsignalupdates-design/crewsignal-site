Status This Week

No new public bargaining recap, complete tentative agreement or ratification timetable was announced for Envoy Flight Attendants this week. CWA-AFA’s November 8 update on the October Dallas session remains the latest detailed account.

Process Signals

  • The committee reported agreement on much of the proposed Reserve language. Airport standby duration and assignments during the 45 minutes after standby remained open. The committee listed no completed tentative agreements from that session.
  • The committee reported proposals for more rest on domestic and international trips and more monthly days off. It was still awaiting the company’s Scheduling proposal when it issued the update.
  • The November 8 update listed talks for the week of November 9, but no subsequent public recap established their outcome. The published calendar identified the weeks of November 30 and January 25 for future sessions.
  • Section 36 sets April 1, 2026 as the first amendable date. The 233 days measure calendar time since amendability, not time at the bargaining table.

CrewSignal Assessment

The public record supports direct successor-contract bargaining, not a completed Reserve section or a move to federal mediation. The remaining Reserve issues matter because they determine how long Flight Attendants can be held at the airport and when they can be assigned flying.
