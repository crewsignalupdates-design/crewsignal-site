Avelo / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.11.06

First-contract mediation · No prior-contract amendability date

Status This Week

No complete first agreement or new ratification timetable was announced during the week. CWA-AFA’s October 16 account of the October 13–14 mediated meetings remains the latest detailed bargaining update.

Process Signals

  • The October account said only a small number of issues remained and that the mediator had directed the parties not to disclose further negotiating details.
  • CWA-AFA’s September 12 newsletter confirmed mediated sessions on September 2 and 8 and section-level tentative agreements on Union Security, Leaves of Absence and Benefits.
  • Agreement on individual sections is not a complete first contract. Final terms, a member vote and implementation remain separate milestones.

CrewSignal Assessment

The October account suggests that the parties had narrowed the remaining issues, but it did not announce a complete settlement. A full tentative agreement and member-voting timetable would mark the next decisive steps.
