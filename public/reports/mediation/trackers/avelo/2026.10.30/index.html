Avelo / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.30

First-contract mediation · No prior-contract amendability date

Status This Week

No complete first agreement or ratification timetable was announced publicly during October 24–30. CWA-AFA’s October 16 account remains the latest verified session report, confirming mediated meetings with management on October 13–14.

Process Signals

  • The October 16 account said only a small number of issues remained and that the federal mediator had directed both parties not to disclose further details about the status of negotiations.
  • CWA-AFA’s September update reported section-level tentative agreements on Union Security, Leaves of Absence and Benefits. Those partial agreements are not a complete first contract.
  • The public record therefore confirms continued mediation without establishing final terms, a ratification schedule or implementation.

CrewSignal Assessment

Avelo’s bargaining appears to have moved into a narrower set of unresolved issues, but the available public information does not establish that a complete agreement has been reached. The next decisive milestone would be a full tentative agreement and a member voting timetable.
