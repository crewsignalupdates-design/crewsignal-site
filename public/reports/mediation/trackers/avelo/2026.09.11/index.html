CrewSignal Assessment

At 1,611 days since certification, Avelo remains the sharpest first-contract test in this tracker. The union’s promised experts and systemwide strength should be evaluated by whether they yield enforceable pay, scheduling, job-security and grievance protections—not by institutional scale alone.

The record does not permit CrewSignal to assign the full delay to CWA-AFA, management or the NMB. It does support an advocacy demand for clearer current reporting and an explanation of what obstacles remain after more than four years without a first agreement.
