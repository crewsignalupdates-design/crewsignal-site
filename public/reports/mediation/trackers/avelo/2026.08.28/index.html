CrewSignal Assessment

A first contract still had not been publicly completed 1,598 days after certification. That is a consequential performance measure because the workgroup remains without a first collectively bargained agreement while the union’s stated multi-carrier advantage is access to experienced negotiators, model language, legal support and institutional leverage.

The certification-to-present clock must not be mislabeled as uninterrupted time at the table, and the available public record does not assign every day of delay to CWA-AFA, Avelo management or the NMB. Even with those limits, the length of the wait and the absence of a durable recent public bargaining account warrant continued scrutiny of both results and transparency.
