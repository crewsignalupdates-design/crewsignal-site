CrewSignal Assessment

A first-contract campaign is a direct test of the multi-carrier model: experienced negotiators, tested language and cross-carrier comparisons can reduce the burden on a newly organized workgroup. The elapsed time since certification is nevertheless substantial, and this week’s public record shows no new proposal exchange, tentative agreement, comprehensive settlement or ratification timetable.

The certification-to-present measure should not be mislabeled as uninterrupted time at the table, and the available sources do not assign all delay to either CWA-AFA or management. It remains a useful measure of how long the workgroup has waited for a first enforceable agreement.
