Omni Air International / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.11.20

Mediated successor-contract negotiations

Status This Week

No new public bargaining recap, revised tentative agreement or vote date was announced this week. The last publicly documented stage was federal mediation toward a successor contract after Flight Attendants rejected a tentative agreement.

Process Signals

  • In July, CWA-AFA said members identified duty-day length as their top concern, alongside pay, scheduling protections and benefits. In August, its committee reported working on revised proposals and expecting to meet the mediator. Those updates did not confirm a November session.
  • ATSG announced on November 10 that it had completed the sale of Omni Air International to OAI Holdings, LLC. The sale announcement did not report a change to Flight Attendant negotiations.

CrewSignal Assessment

The ownership change and the contract talks are separate developments. No new package or member vote was publicly announced. The absence of a public recap does not show that negotiations stopped.
