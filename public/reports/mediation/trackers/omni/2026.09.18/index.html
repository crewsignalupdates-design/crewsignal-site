Process Signals

  • The public negotiations timeline still shows the January tentative agreement, February rejection and April member survey as the most recent formal milestones in the renewed process.
  • The NMB weekly report did not identify a new Omni docket, reassignment, ratification or closure. That does not negate existing mediator involvement; an already-open case need not appear every week.
  • Omni’s 2024 strike-authorization vote remains a bargaining-pressure and solidarity signal, not an independent right to strike. The renewed process must still produce a member-responsive agreement or proceed through the Railway Labor Act’s later statutory steps.

CrewSignal Assessment

Omni is a direct test of whether CWA-AFA’s professional negotiators, attorneys and cross-carrier resources can convert a rejected tentative agreement into a materially stronger second result. At 1,564 days since the first session, process activity alone is no longer an adequate measure.

The next public update should identify whether the mediator meeting occurred, which proposals were revised after the member survey and what changed in response to the 100–73 rejection. Without that record, the renewed bargaining strategy remains difficult to evaluate.
