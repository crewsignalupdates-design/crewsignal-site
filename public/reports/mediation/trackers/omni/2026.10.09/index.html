Omni Air International / CWA-AFA Mediation

Week ending 2026.10.09

Status This Week

Omni’s successor-contract negotiations continue following the rejection of the earlier tentative agreement. No dated October 3–9 public announcement of a replacement agreement or ratification timetable was identified.

Process Signals

  • The July 21 CWA-AFA update described preparations for revised proposals following member feedback. Duty-period length was a leading concern, alongside compensation, scheduling, and benefits.
  • The August 16 update said the negotiations team was developing proposals and expected to meet with the mediator in the following weeks. That announcement is not confirmation that a subsequent meeting occurred or that revised terms were settled.
  • Duty limits, pay, and scheduling remain central to the revised proposals described by CWA-AFA. Those priorities are distinct from a complete agreement members can review and vote on.

CrewSignal Assessment

The public record supports a continued effort to revise the rejected settlement, but does not establish the contents or timing of a replacement agreement. The next meaningful milestone is a dated account of renewed bargaining or the release of a complete revised tentative agreement.
