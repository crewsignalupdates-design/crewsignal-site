Republic / Mesa

Week ending 2026.09.18

Status Summary

Republic / Mesa remained in the representation phase following the National Mediation Board’s September 1 finding that the two carriers constituted a single transportation system for the Flight Attendants craft or class. The determination converted CR-7275 into representation case R-7690 and continued the investigation rather than issuing the final combined certification.

The decision identified 2,962 covered Flight Attendants: 625 at Mesa and 2,337 at Republic. IBT and CWA-AFA had filed jointly, but CWA-AFA told the Board that it was not acting as an intervenor and agreed that its Mesa certification should transfer to IBT. The Board allowed any intervenor 30 days from September 1 to file an application supported by at least 50 percent of the combined craft or class.

The NMB relied on common ownership, common management, centralized labor and personnel operations, phased JCBA implementation, an integrated seniority list, common policies, operating-certificate transition work, common public communications, and Mesa Flight Attendant movement toward Republic uniforms. The Board expressly found substantial integration despite the absence of some public-facing indicators associated with larger branded airlines.

CrewSignal identified no later public certification or dismissal dated through September 18. The case therefore illustrated that a single-transportation-system finding advances a representation proceeding but does not automatically produce either a representation vote or immediate final certification.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch R-7690 through the October 1 intervention deadline for an additional application, showing of interest, certification, election authorization, or dismissal.
  • If no qualifying intervenor appears, watch whether the NMB transfers or issues the combined certification to IBT without an election.
  • If a qualifying intervenor appears, identify the organization, the form of its showing, the eligible combined craft or class, and the NMB’s chosen representation method.
  • How will IBT’s prospective combined certification translate into dues, grievance authority, bargaining governance, elections, financial accountability, and day-to-day representation for former Mesa members?
  • Watch implementation of the JCBA and integrated seniority list for effects on payroll, boarding pay, uniforms, training, benefits, scheduling, bases, furlough protection, and aircraft assignments.
  • Which documented outcomes show that the joint-union transition protected Flight Attendants better than a contested election or unilateral absorption process?
