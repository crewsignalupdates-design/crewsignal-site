Republic / Mesa

Week ending 2026.10.02

Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Flight Attendant record was identified for September 26–October 2. The governing carry-forward development is the NMB’s September 1 single-transportation-system determination, not a representation election result.

That order allowed intervenors 30 days to file an application supported by a showing of interest of at least 50 percent of the combined Flight Attendant craft or class. The passage of that period does not itself establish who filed or decide representation. CWA-AFA’s stated agreement to transfer its certification to IBT remained a party position rather than a completed Board disposition in the dated record used here.

Integration Dashboard

  • The NMB’s September 1 decision established a single transportation system for Flight Attendant representation and moved the applications from CR-7275 into Case R-7690.
  • IBT represented pre-merger Republic Flight Attendants, while CWA-AFA represented pre-merger Mesa Flight Attendants; the September order kept existing certifications effective pending further Board action.
  • The JCBA was ratified on January 22, 2026, and the unions presented an integrated seniority list on April 21, but the September decision described further contract implementation as ongoing.
  • The same decision recorded boarding pay effective July 22 and Republic schedule-adjustment changes implemented in August.
  • Mesa Flight Attendants had begun transitioning to Republic uniforms; the September decision did not establish that the rollout was complete.
  • The operating-certificate process remained distinct from the NMB representation proceeding; management’s integration timetable was a forecast, not a completed FAA certification.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Follow the NMB’s next action in Flight Attendant Case R-7690 and distinguish any certification, election authorization or dismissal from the already-issued single-system determination.
  • Look for dated implementation notices confirming how the integrated seniority list is applied to bidding, bases and assignments, rather than equating list delivery with operational use.
  • Check remaining training, reserve, payroll and benefit transitions, including whether contractual protections and any exceptions apply to particular aircraft or employee transfers. Track the announced year-end phaseout of Mesa-uniform issuance without assuming completion.
  • Compare management’s operating-certificate target of no later than June 2028, as recited in the September decision, with actual FAA milestones, and track provision-by-provision implementation without assuming every work rule is already harmonized.
