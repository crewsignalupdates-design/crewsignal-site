Integration Tracker

Republic / Mesa

Week ending 2026.11.13

Status Summary

The National Mediation Board (NMB) issued a new Republic / Mesa representation decision on November 13, extending International Brotherhood of Teamsters (IBT) certification to the combined Mechanics and Related Employees craft or class. That is a separate craft-specific milestone, not a new Flight Attendant certification or evidence that Flight Attendant contract implementation is complete.

No new Flight Attendant implementation milestone was identified in the public records reviewed. The October 13 Flight Attendant certification remains the governing representation milestone; public evidence of provision-by-provision execution remains incomplete.

Integration Dashboard

  • Flight Attendant representation: The October 13 order extended IBT certification R-6225 across the combined Flight Attendants craft or class, extinguished CWA-AFA certification R-6499, and closed R-7690. It did not certify completed payroll, scheduling, seniority-list use, or operating integration.
  • Other-craft representation: In 54 NMB No. 4, the Board extended IBT certification R-7676 across the combined Mechanics and Related Employees craft or class and closed R-7697. No intervenor applied. The Board rejected the carrier’s request for a further election, applying its certification-extension policy to the differently sized groups. This order does not alter the Flight Attendant disposition.
  • Contract and seniority execution: The September 1 NMB record described January 22 ratification of the joint collective bargaining agreement (JCBA), April 21 presentation of an integrated seniority list, and continuing implementation. It also recorded specific earlier pay and scheduling changes. Those findings do not establish that every provision or every use of the seniority list has since been completed.
  • Operating integration: Republic’s October 29 release reported progress in maintenance and supply-chain integration and relocation of Mesa’s operations center to Carmel. Management expected the integration process to conclude in the first half of 2028. That forecast is not an issued Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) single operating certificate or proof of completed Flight Attendant implementation.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Which dated records demonstrate integrated seniority-list use in bids, bases, and assignments, rather than presentation of the list alone?
  • What evidence confirms actual reserve, scheduling, boarding-pay and payroll execution, training, benefits, uniforms, and grievance administration under the joint agreement?
  • How are IBT steward and member-service arrangements reaching legacy-Mesa Flight Attendants, and which FAA milestones demonstrate progress toward a single operating certificate?
