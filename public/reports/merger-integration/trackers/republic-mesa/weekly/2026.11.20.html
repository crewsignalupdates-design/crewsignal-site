Integration Tracker

Republic / Mesa

Week ending 2026.11.20

Status Summary

The International Brotherhood of Teamsters (IBT) remains the certified representative for the combined Republic / Mesa Flight Attendant group under the National Mediation Board’s October 13 decision. The public records reviewed did not identify a new milestone showing that the integrated seniority list was used for common bids or that the joint contract rollout was complete.

Integration Dashboard

  • Representation: The October 13 order extended IBT’s Flight Attendant certification and ended CWA-AFA’s former Mesa certification. The November 13 extension for Mechanics and Related Employees was a separate decision.
  • Contract and seniority: The joint agreement was ratified on January 22, and the unions presented an integrated seniority list on April 21. The September National Mediation Board record described July 22 boarding-pay changes and an August schedule-adjustment change at Republic. Those were specific steps, not proof the agreement was fully implemented or that both groups began bidding from the same list.
  • Operating integration: On October 29, Republic reported relocating Mesa’s operations center to Carmel, Indiana, and said work toward one Federal Aviation Administration operating certificate continued. Its first-half 2028 integration target is not an FAA issuance date.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • When will common bids, base awards or assignments first use the integrated seniority list?
  • Which dated notices show remaining contract terms in use and explain how former Mesa Flight Attendants access steward and grievance services?
  • What steps remain before the FAA issues a single operating certificate?
