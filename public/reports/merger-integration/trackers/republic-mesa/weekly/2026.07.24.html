Republic / Mesa

Week ending 2026.07.24

Editor’s Note: This Merger & Integration hub tracks airline consolidation signals, commercial partnerships, and representation-level implications. Status Summary

Republic / Mesa reached a concrete contract-administration milestone on July 22 when contractual boarding pay began for the combined flight-attendant group. CWA-AFA publicly credited cooperation with IBT for the implementation, and the previously published schedule had identified July 22 as the date requiring computer programming at both Mesa and Republic.

The JCBA provision pays 30 minutes per boarding at 50% of the applicable hourly rate, including qualifying reboardings, and places that pay above guarantee. The implementation is a measurable payroll and technology integration step, but it does not complete the broader merger process. CrewSignal identified no new exact-week public pilot JCBA, integrated-seniority-list, operating-certificate, or NMB representation milestone.

Integration Dashboard

  • Contractual boarding pay began for Mesa and Republic flight attendants on July 22, 2026.
  • The implementation depended on programming and payroll tracking at both Mesa and Republic, making it a concrete test of cross-carrier contract administration.
  • The JCBA provides 30 minutes of pay per boarding at 50% of the flight attendant’s applicable hourly rate, including qualifying reboardings, and pays the credit above guarantee.
  • CWA-AFA publicly attributed the implementation in part to cooperation between CWA-AFA and IBT in holding management to the negotiated timetable.
  • No new exact-week public update resolved the remaining transition questions involving trip rig, additional days off, or uniform allotment.
  • No new exact-week public pilot JCBA, integrated-seniority-list, or single-operating-certificate milestone was identified.
  • The NMB’s FY2026 determinations page added an unrelated railroad certification dated July 23 and no exact-week Republic / Mesa air-carrier determination.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch initial pay statements for accurate boarding-pay credits, reboardings, above-guarantee treatment, and any retroactive corrections caused by programming or payroll issues.
  • Watch for resolution of the remaining flight-attendant implementation items and for the point at which Mesa-specific contract administration gives way to Republic operating processes.
  • Watch the pilot JCBA and integrated-seniority-list sequence, including any movement toward the merger agreement’s arbitration pathway and the eventual single operating certificate.
  • Watch CWA-AFA and IBT coordination on representation. An NMB representation investigation would require an application by employees or a representative organization and a sufficient showing of interest; common ownership and carrier notice alone do not initiate the case.
