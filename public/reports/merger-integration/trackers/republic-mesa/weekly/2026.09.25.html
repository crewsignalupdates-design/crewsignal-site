Status Summary

Republic / Mesa remained in the post-single-system representation phase throughout the reporting week. The National Mediation Board’s September 1 determination had converted the matter into R-7690, identified a combined craft or class of 2,962 Flight Attendants, and allowed any intervenor 30 calendar days to submit an application supported by at least 50 percent of the combined group.

The NMB activity report for September 21–25 showed no election authorization, ballot instructions, certification, dismissal, or other public disposition in R-7690. The intervention period therefore remained open as of the September 25 reporting cutoff.

IBT and CWA-AFA had filed the original matter jointly, while CWA-AFA told the Board that it was not intervening and supported transfer of its Mesa certification to IBT. A single-transportation-system finding advanced the representation case, but it did not itself create a vote or issue the final combined certification.

CrewSignal identified no new exact-week company integration announcement, JCBA implementation notice, seniority-list update, or public union-governance explanation changing the Republic / Mesa record.
