Status Summary

Alaska / Hawaiian did not produce a new exact-week company release, SEC filing, independently reported integration milestone, public JCBA or integrated-seniority-list update, or NMB representation determination during the week ending July 31, 2026.

The public baseline therefore remains the July 21 to July 24 cluster of developments: completion of the single passenger service system, the future Hawaiian-branded Boeing 737-800 Neighbor Island fleet plan, the nearer-term Alaska-branded 737 operation using Alaska crews, and the CWA-AFA Joint Negotiating Committee update on insurance benefits and training. CrewSignal Watch Points
