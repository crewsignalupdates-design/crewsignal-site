Week ending 2026.08.14

Status Summary

The principal exact-week Flight Attendant development was a grievance announced by CWA-AFA on August 14 for pre-merger Hawaiian Flight Attendants assigned to the Boeing 787-9. The union said the current service model imposed an unsustainable pace and reported that management had instructed crews to “make it work.” The grievance asks management either to provide the resources needed to operate the service model or to make realistic service adjustments reflecting the staffing provided. The filing documents a legacy Hawaiian workload and contract-enforcement dispute; it is not a combined-carrier JCBA or seniority-integration milestone.

On August 12, CWA-AFA posted a recap of its first pre-merger Alaska reserve webinar. The recap addressed practical contractual rights and responsibilities for legacy Alaska reserve Flight Attendants, including scheduling limits and assignment administration. It likewise remained a legacy-contract education item rather than implementation of common Alaska / Hawaiian reserve rules.

CrewSignal identified no new exact-week company or SEC integration announcement, public JCBA agreement, integrated-seniority-list implementation step, NMB Flight Attendant representation action, or additional single-operating-certificate milestone. The latest public joint-negotiations report remained the July 24 update, and the next announced bargaining session remained scheduled for August 24–26.

Integration Dashboard

  • The August 14 Boeing 787-9 grievance applied to pre-merger Hawaiian Flight Attendants and challenged the staffing and pace of the current inflight service model.
  • CWA-AFA asked management either to provide the resources required by the service model or to make service adjustments that reflect the staffing actually provided.
  • The grievance tests legacy Hawaiian contract enforcement while the two Flight Attendant groups remain without a fully implemented common agreement.
  • The August 12 reserve webinar recap applied to pre-merger Alaska Flight Attendants and addressed rights and responsibilities under the legacy Alaska agreement.
  • Legacy-group education and grievance enforcement remain necessary even though CWA-AFA represents both Flight Attendant groups.
  • The NMB has already found a single transportation system for the combined Flight Attendants craft or class and certified CWA-AFA as the combined representative.
  • A new representation election or authorization-card campaign is not required merely to preserve the existing combined CWA-AFA certification.
  • Same-union continuity avoids a contest between different incumbent representatives, but it does not by itself create common work rules, benefits, scheduling, bases, seniority administration, or workload protections.
  • No new exact-week public JCBA or integrated-seniority-list implementation milestone was identified.
  • The next publicly announced joint-bargaining session remained scheduled for August 24–26.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Does same-union continuity produce faster and more enforceable common protections when each legacy group still depends on separate contract enforcement during integration?
  • Watch the Boeing 787-9 grievance for a settlement, adjustment to staffing or service standards, escalation, or a published contractual disposition.
  • Watch the August 24–26 bargaining session for movement on insurance benefits, training, reserve, seniority, domiciles, and other remaining JCBA subjects.
