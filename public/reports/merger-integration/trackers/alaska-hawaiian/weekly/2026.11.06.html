Week ending 2026.11.06

Historical window: October 31–November 6, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Cutoff: November 7, 2026, 07:59:59 UTC.

Status Summary

CWA-AFA’s November 6 notice announces aircraft-specific 787-10 negotiations under the pre-merger Hawaiian CBA. The notice is directed to pre-merger Hawaiian Flight Attendants, reports receipt of the company’s intent letter on September 8, and schedules talks with management for November 19–20. This aircraft bargaining is separate from the Alaska/Hawaiian JCBA and does not establish overall contract ratification, integrated seniority-list use, FAA certification, or combined work rules.

The October 30 notice scheduled reserve-survey review for November 6–9. By the November 6 cutoff, the scheduled period had opened, but the reviewed sources do not establish that the review actually began, was completed, or produced findings or proposals.

Integration Dashboard

  • 787-10 scope: The notice invokes Hawaiian CBA §27.C.2. That provision covers wages, rules and working conditions for the other aircraft type in the International operation. It provides for talks fifteen days after written notice unless mutually agreed otherwise; the notice does not explain any timing arrangement. Section 27.C.3 requires agreement before the new aircraft enters service.
  • Committee: The notice’s body names Martin Gusman, Jeff Fuke and Bianca McKee, joined by senior staff negotiator Paula Mastrangelo and chair Tim Green. No negotiated 787-10 terms are announced.
  • Joint bargaining: The October 30 account remains carry-forward context. Its individual Seniority and retiree tentative agreements do not establish overall JCBA ratification, effectiveness or integrated-list delivery. The November 16–18 bargaining session remains future at cutoff.
  • Legacy-Alaska implementation: The scheduling recap was published November 3 about an October 27 meeting; the reserve-webinar recap was published November 4 about the October 21 event. These address legacy-group scheduling and reserve issues, not combined work-rule implementation.
  • FAA and NMB: The reviewed in-window union notices establish no new certification or representation disposition. Aircraft-specific talks and a JNC meeting announcement are not regulatory milestones.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Look for a dated account of reserve-survey review before reporting findings or completion. The October 30 source records more than 1,000 responses, not a negotiated reserve agreement.
  • After the scheduled sessions, distinguish any 787-10 agreement from JCBA bargaining progress, ratification, seniority-list delivery and operational implementation.
  • Keep legacy-Alaska scheduling fixes and reserve guidance attributed to their own contract and workgroup. Any combined-system milestone needs separate evidence.
