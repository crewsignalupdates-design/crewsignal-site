Status Summary

Alaska / Hawaiian produced two concrete exact-week integration signals. On July 14, Alaska Airlines, Inc., which includes Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, announced coordinated seasonal network changes that add Honolulu service from Boise and Spokane, expand peak Honolulu–Las Vegas flying, and redeploy aircraft from discontinued seasonal Honolulu–Auckland service toward stronger domestic Hawaii demand.

A July 15 company feature also documented the Alaska Hawaiian app as a shared customer platform across both airlines. Guests can retain an Alaska- or Hawaiian-themed experience while managing itineraries, checking in, accessing boarding passes, tracking Atmos Rewards, and using travel-day tools across the combined network. No new exact-week public pilot or flight-attendant JCBA, seniority, or representation milestone was identified.

Integration Dashboard

  • The Alaska Hawaiian app now brings itineraries, check-in, boarding passes, real-time updates, and trip-management tools for both airlines into one customer platform.
  • The shared app preserves a choice of Alaska- or Hawaiian-branded presentation while supporting Atmos Rewards balances, status tracking, and money-or-points searches across Alaska, Hawaiian, and partner airlines.
  • Alaska will add daily seasonal Honolulu–Boise service and weekly seasonal Honolulu–Spokane service using Alaska-branded Boeing 737 aircraft.
  • Hawaiian will add three weekly Honolulu–Las Vegas flights during peak periods, increasing the route from three to four daily flights on those dates.
  • Hawaiian will not resume seasonal Honolulu–Auckland service, and the company said the aircraft will be repositioned to support additional domestic Hawaii flying.
  • The company projected that the announced additions and increased California flying will raise seasonal Hawaii capacity by 3% year over year and by as much as 6% during peak holiday periods.
  • No new exact-week public JCBA, seniority-integration, or representation milestone was identified for the Alaska and Hawaiian pilot or flight-attendant groups.
