Alaska / Hawaiian

Week ending 2026.09.25

Editor’s Note: CrewSignal uses public records to compare how unions structure, negotiate, communicate, and protect Flight Attendants during mergers and representation proceedings. Status Summary

A later-published recap of the September 23 Alaska / Hawaiian CWA-AFA town hall documented several exact-week integration concerns. Union representatives said the Joint Negotiating Committee hoped Interest-based Bargaining training would accelerate difficult combined-contract sections, identified year-round base-transfer access as a subject worth evaluating for the JCBA, and asked members to report non-revenue travel and seniority inconsistencies so systemic integration issues could be addressed with management.

The same town hall linked post-merger route shifts to shorter layovers and continued fatigue concerns, while reserve, scheduling, timekeeping, and pay questions remained governed by legacy Alaska rules. Those issues show that combined certification has not yet produced a common Flight Attendant operating framework.

A later-published Hotel Committee recap documented September 23 and September 24 quarterly business reviews for the pre-merger Alaska and pre-merger Hawaiian operations. Crew Hotels managed both operations, but the procurement vendor API continued to maintain separate accounts specializing in each legacy airline. That is a concrete example of centralized company oversight operating alongside continued legacy-system separation.

CrewSignal identified no new exact-week tentative agreement, integrated-seniority-list implementation milestone, additional single-operating-certificate development, or Flight Attendant representation action. The NMB activity report for September 21–25 contained no Alaska / Hawaiian representation application, election authorization, certification, or dismissal.

Integration Dashboard

  • The September 23 town hall documented continuing member questions about scheduling, reserve administration, base transfers, non-revenue seniority treatment, and fatigue.
  • The Joint Negotiating Committee expected Interest-based Bargaining training to help address complex combined-contract sections more efficiently.
  • Year-round base-transfer access was identified as a possible JCBA subject rather than an implemented combined benefit.
  • CWA-AFA continued collecting reports of non-revenue travel and seniority inconsistencies as potential systemic integration concerns.
  • The town hall linked post-merger route shifts to shorter layovers and increased fatigue concerns.
  • Crew Hotels managed both Alaska and Hawaiian hotel operations during the reporting week.
  • API continued to maintain separate legacy-airline accounts, demonstrating partial administrative integration rather than one fully unified system.
  • The NMB had already certified CWA-AFA for the combined Flight Attendants craft or class.
  • Same-union continuity avoided a contested representation election but did not itself harmonize scheduling, reserve, hotel, benefit, base, or seniority administration.
  • No new exact-week JCBA tentative agreement or integrated-seniority-list implementation milestone was identified.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch whether Interest-based Bargaining produces faster resolution of difficult sections while preserving member transparency and enforceable protections.
  • Watch whether the JCBA creates more frequent base-transfer opportunities and resolves systemic seniority treatment across legacy operations.
  • Watch whether hotel procurement, transportation, reporting systems, and vendor accounts move from shared oversight toward one common Flight Attendant process.
  • Watch whether post-merger route changes and short layovers produce negotiated pairing, rest, fatigue, or staffing protections.
  • Watch for movement on Seniority and for clear member-facing explanations of effects on bidding, bases, training, and aircraft assignments.
  • Does same-union continuity produce a sufficiently timely common agreement when multiple legacy systems remain operational after combined certification?
