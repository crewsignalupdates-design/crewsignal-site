Integration Tracker

Alaska / Hawaiian

Week ending 2026.11.13

Status Summary

Alaska and Hawaiian pilot leaders approved a tentative joint collective bargaining agreement (JCBA) on November 10 for advancement to the combined pilot membership for ratification. That is a pilot bargaining milestone, not a ratified agreement or a Flight Attendant integration outcome. CWA-AFA’s November 11 response welcomed the pilot progress while stating that its own joint negotiations still have important work ahead.

The Flight Attendant reserve-survey follow-through remains unresolved in the public record reviewed. The October 30 bargaining update scheduled intensive survey review for November 6–9; no dated account confirming completion, published findings or resulting reserve proposals was identified. Neither the scheduled review nor the previously reported response count establishes agreed or implemented reserve protections.

Integration Dashboard

  • Joint bargaining: CWA-AFA’s November 13 recap lists the next joint negotiations for November 17–19. Its October 30 notice had listed November 16–18. These remain announced future dates, not completed sessions or negotiated results.
  • Aircraft-specific bargaining: The November 13 recap repeats the November 6 notice about pre-merger Hawaiian 787-10 negotiations. That earlier notice schedules management talks for November 19–20. This is a separate aircraft-bargaining track, not evidence of a completed Flight Attendant JCBA.
  • Seniority and implementation: The October 30 notice reported a tentative agreement on the Seniority section, alongside retiree provisions. A section-level agreement does not establish an overall ratified JCBA, delivery or operational use of an integrated seniority list, or common work rules. No new Flight Attendant implementation milestone was identified in the public sources reviewed.
  • Legacy-group administration: Council 43’s November 11 notice reports unresolved bid-submission problems affecting a limited number of pre-merger Hawaiian Flight Attendants. Its workaround guidance is not evidence of a combined bidding-system rollout or a permanent fix.
  • Regulatory distinction: The pilot tentative agreement does not itself establish a new Federal Aviation Administration certification or Flight Attendant representation result.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • What dated account establishes whether the reserve-survey review was completed, what members prioritized and which proposals followed?
  • What does the next joint-bargaining account establish beyond the announced session dates and earlier section-level tentative agreements?
  • Do the 787-10 talks produce aircraft-specific terms, and how are those terms kept distinct from JCBA ratification and combined-workgroup implementation?
  • When will separate evidence establish integrated seniority-list use, common scheduling rules and resolved bidding problems rather than plans or workarounds?
