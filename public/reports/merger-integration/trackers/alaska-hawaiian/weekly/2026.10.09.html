Week ending 2026.10.09

Status Summary

The clearest new joint-contract development during October 3–9 was member participation: on October 6, CWA-AFA opened a reserve survey for both pre-merger groups to inform the Joint Negotiating Committee’s proposal. The accompanying educational material compared the legacy reserve programs. Opening the survey did not settle a reserve section or implement common work rules.

A separate October 5 scheduling recap reported on the pre-merger Alaska committee’s September 29 meeting. It described legacy staffing pressure, pay and fatigue-processing concerns, and system work. The event preceded this reporting window, but the account became public within it. The recap’s operational figures describe earlier months in the legacy Alaska operation, rather than October performance or results for the combined workforce.

Combined CWA-AFA certification and the single operating certificate remain completed carry-forward milestones. No new overall JCBA ratification, implemented integrated seniority list or representation disposition was established by the dated public material located for this week.

Integration Dashboard

  • The October 6 survey invited participation across both legacy Flight Attendant groups, rather than only current reserves.
  • The survey was bargaining preparation, not a member vote on a tentative agreement.
  • The October 5 recap attributed Seattle’s 97.7 percent reserve utilization to August data for the legacy Alaska operation.
  • The same recap identified continuing pay-audit and fatigue-processing concerns within legacy scheduling administration.
  • The September bargaining record still distinguished unresolved Training, Grievance and economic subjects from prior tentative sections.
  • The NMB’s combined certification did not itself merge the two Flight Attendant contracts or implement the seniority list.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch for the survey’s announced October 20 noon-Pacific deadline, subsequent member feedback and a reserve proposal.
  • Watch for a report of the October 14–15 bargaining-method training and the October 26–28 negotiating session, both still future at this cutoff.
  • Watch for confirmed launch and scope of the AICS vacancy/transfer-bidding work and Crew Access changes described in the October 5 recap.
  • Watch whether scheduling, rest and pay concerns produce enforceable changes and whether any changes apply to one legacy group or the combined craft.
