Status Summary

In a September 14 update describing bargaining conducted during the reporting week, the Alaska / Hawaiian CWA-AFA Joint Negotiating Committee said it met management in Seattle from September 8 through September 10. The parties exchanged responses on Grievance and Training, and management presented a new proposal on Performance Based Pay and Retirement. The union responded with proposals addressing legacy retiree pass privileges and retiree health insurance.

The committee said the remaining sections were complex because the two established agreements cover different operations and work rules. The parties agreed to explore Interest-based Bargaining, with National Mediation Board-led training scheduled for October 14–15. The reserve survey was being finalized for release in October, and the next bargaining session was scheduled for October 26–28.

The NMB mediator’s role in Interest-based Bargaining is separate from the Board’s representation function. In a representation matter, an application initiates the investigation; the Board may later use a secret ballot or another appropriate method if representation must be determined. A transaction need not carry a formal merger label to fall within the Manual’s broader transaction definition, but no alliance, common ownership arrangement, or commercial relationship automatically establishes a single transportation system.

Alaska / Hawaiian’s Flight Attendant certification remained settled in favor of CWA-AFA. The week’s developments concerned JCBA method and substance, not a new representation dispute.
