Integration Tracker

Alaska / Hawaiian

Week ending 2026.11.20

Status Summary

The public record still shows two separate Flight Attendant bargaining tracks. CWA-AFA listed November 17–19 for joint contract talks and November 19–20 for pre-merger Hawaiian 787-10 talks. No public recap reviewed confirmed whether either session took place or what it produced.

The union’s November 20 recap shared inflight-service advocacy, including a call to test service with normal crew staffing. It did not report a bargaining result or a change to the rules crews work under.

Integration Dashboard

  • Joint bargaining: The October 30 recap remains the latest account of a completed joint session located. It reported a tentative Seniority section and two retiree letters, not an overall joint contract or an integrated seniority list in use.
  • Reserve: The joint committee reported more than 1,000 survey responses and planned to review them November 6–9. No dated confirmation of the review, published results, or resulting Reserve proposal was identified.
  • Aircraft-specific bargaining: The 787-10 notice cites Section 27.C.2 of the pre-merger Hawaiian agreement. Any aircraft-specific terms would be separate from a joint contract for both Flight Attendant groups.
  • Implementation: The public material reviewed did not establish new combined scheduling, work rules, seniority-list use, or a Flight Attendant ratification result.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • What did each bargaining session produce, if it took place?
  • Which Reserve priorities or proposals emerge from the survey?
  • When will crews see an integrated seniority list used in bids or assignments, rather than a tentative seniority provision alone?
