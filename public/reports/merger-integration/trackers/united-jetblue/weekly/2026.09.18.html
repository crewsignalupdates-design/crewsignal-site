United / JetBlue

Week ending 2026.09.18

Editor’s Note: CrewSignal uses public records to compare how unions structure, negotiate, communicate, and protect Flight Attendants during mergers and representation proceedings. It may raise questions supported by the record; it will not fabricate facts or state conclusions the evidence cannot establish. This report is informational and does not constitute legal, investment, or merger advice.

Status Summary

CrewSignal identified no exact-week Blue Sky ownership, common-control, shared-management, centralized-labor, or NMB Flight Attendant representation development. JetBlue continued to describe Blue Sky as a loyalty and customer collaboration between two separate airlines. Customers could book eligible flights operated by either carrier through JetBlue channels, but mixed JetBlue-operated and United-operated connecting journeys still required separate itineraries, and JetBlue stated that the partnership was not a codeshare.

Two United-only Flight Attendant developments illustrated the continued separation of the workforces. On September 17, United CWA-AFA published benefit guidance concerning automatic Group Universal Life enrollment for Flight Attendants returning from leaves of absence. The union also welcomed a new-hire class assigned to United domiciles at Chicago, San Francisco, and Washington Dulles.

Neither development established common United / JetBlue Flight Attendant benefits, hiring, bases, training, scheduling, contracts, seniority, or labor administration. United Flight Attendants remained represented by CWA-AFA, while JetBlue Inflight Crewmembers remained represented by TWU Local 579. No public joint-union protocol, representation application, authorization-card campaign, or single-transportation-system determination was identified.

CrewSignal is beginning a separate Blue Sky Single-Carrier Watch to measure the partnership against the NMB’s published factors, explain what could initiate a Flight Attendant representation case, and compare the path with Alaska / Hawaiian, Allegiant / Sun Country, and Republic / Mesa.

Integration Dashboard

  • Blue Sky remained a customer-facing and loyalty collaboration between airlines that JetBlue publicly described as separate.
  • Eligible United-operated flights could be sold through JetBlue channels, and reciprocal loyalty earning, redemption, and benefits were available.
  • Mixed JetBlue-operated and United-operated connecting journeys still required separate itineraries.
  • JetBlue stated that the partnership was not a codeshare and that the operating carrier remained identified.
  • United’s September 17 life-insurance guidance applied only to United Flight Attendants returning from leave.
  • United’s newest Flight Attendant class was assigned to United domiciles and did not establish a combined Blue Sky workforce.
  • United Flight Attendants remained represented by CWA-AFA, while JetBlue Inflight Crewmembers remained represented by TWU Local 579.
  • No public CWA-AFA–TWU Flight Attendant transition protocol, JCBA, seniority process, representation application, or NMB determination was identified.
  • The NMB Manual defines a covered transaction more broadly than a conventional corporate merger label, but commercial collaboration alone does not automatically establish a single transportation system.
  • A representation matter begins with an application, and carrier notice or public speculation does not substitute for that filing.
  • Any organization or individual may seek a single-transportation-system determination supported by evidence of representation or a showing of interest.
  • The NMB would examine present facts such as combined routes, standardized uniforms, common marketing, integrated scheduling or dispatching, centralized labor and personnel functions, common management, a combined workforce, and common or overlapping ownership.
  • After a single-transportation-system finding, every applicant must establish support from at least 50 percent of the combined Flight Attendant craft or class.
  • Incumbent representatives may rely on current-representation evidence, while a filer representing none of the covered employees must use valid authorization cards.
  • A single-system finding and qualifying showing can lead to an election or another NMB-approved representation method, but a vote is not automatic.
  • Existing certifications remain effective until the NMB issues a new certification or dismissal.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch whether Blue Sky remains limited to customer-facing cooperation or develops centralized labor functions, common management, integrated essential operations, a combined workforce, or changes in ownership and control.
  • Watch the planned single mixed-carrier itinerary capability, Paisly expansion, reciprocal benefits, and United’s contemplated JFK access without assuming that customer integration resolves the NMB inquiry.
  • Will CWA-AFA and TWU Local 579 establish and publish a joint Flight Attendant protocol before a representation dispute arises or Blue Sky materially deepens?
  • Watch for any NMB application and identify who files, which organizations intervene, what evidence incumbents use, and whether a nonincumbent begins collecting authorization cards.
  • What direct role would United and JetBlue Flight Attendants have in choosing the long-term representative and evaluating competing union governance structures?
