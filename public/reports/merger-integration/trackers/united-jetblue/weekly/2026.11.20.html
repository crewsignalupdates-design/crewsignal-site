Integration Tracker

United / JetBlue

Week ending 2026.11.20

Status Summary

No new Blue Sky customer launch or Flight Attendant integration milestone was identified in the public records reviewed. Existing cooperation lets customers book eligible flights across the two airlines’ channels and use certain reciprocal loyalty benefits. Those services do not establish a combined workforce.

Integration Dashboard

  • Customer services: The airlines announced cross-sales on February 10 and reciprocal loyalty benefits on May 14. The original announcement described an interline arrangement, not a codeshare. A single connecting ticket using flights operated by both carriers and United’s planned JFK access were announced as later phases; no new launch of either was identified.
  • Non-air products: JetBlue reported that Paisly started distributing United car rentals in July. Its third-quarter filing projected hotels and travel insurance by year-end, then flight-and-hotel packages and cruises through MileagePlus Travel in the first half of 2027. The car-rental rollout is customer-facing, not a change in Flight Attendant work.
  • Crew and representation: The public records reviewed did not establish shared Flight Attendant management, bargaining, scheduling or qualifications, mixed crews, or a transfer of protected work. No United/JetBlue Flight Attendant application or disposition was identified in the available National Mediation Board records; the public indexes are not a complete filing docket.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Have the airlines begun selling connecting trips using flights operated by both carriers on one ticket?
  • Have United’s planned JFK flights opened for sale, or have additional Paisly services launched?
  • What direct evidence, if any, shows shared crew operations, labor relations, or a Flight Attendant representation proceeding?
