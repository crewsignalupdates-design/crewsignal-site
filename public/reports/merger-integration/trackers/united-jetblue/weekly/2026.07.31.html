Status Summary

United / JetBlue received an exact-week corporate update through JetBlue's July 28 second-quarter results. JetBlue said Blue Sky had advanced through reciprocal loyalty benefits and described the collaboration as making ongoing progress within the JetForward strategy.

The filing did not announce a new discrete Blue Sky launch, ownership or governance change, common-control arrangement, or workforce integration milestone. Reciprocal loyalty benefits therefore remained the latest implemented phase, while connected interline ticketing, additional Paisly-powered products, and contemplated United access at JFK remained future elements of a commercial collaboration between separately managed airlines.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch implementation details for United's contemplated JFK access beginning as early as 2027 and any related airport-process coordination.
  • Watch the JetBlue pilot grievance and related litigation over Blue Sky for any ruling, settlement, or operational limitation.
  • Watch the NMB distinction carefully. A formal corporate-merger label is not required for every transaction covered by the Board's merger procedures, but commercial cooperation alone does not establish a single transportation system; a representation case also requires an application by employees or a representative organization, the required showing of interest, and present integration facts.
