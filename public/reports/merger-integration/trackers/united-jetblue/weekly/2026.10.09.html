United / JetBlue

Week ending 2026.10.09

Editor’s Note: CrewSignal uses public records to compare how unions structure, negotiate, communicate, and protect Flight Attendants during mergers and representation proceedings. Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Blue Sky labor-integration or representation record was identified for October 3–9. United’s October 8 employee-facing notices instead provide additional evidence of its own staffing and contract-benefits administration.

On October 8, CWA-AFA welcomed 79 new United Flight Attendants and separately reported on benefits-volunteer training held during the preceding week. The training addressed United’s 2026–2031 agreement. Both notices concerned United-specific staffing and contract administration.

Integration Dashboard

  • Blue Sky’s established customer-facing features included cross-carrier sales and reciprocal loyalty benefits; these features do not by themselves establish a combined Flight Attendant workforce.
  • United’s five-year Flight Attendant agreement took effect May 31, 2026, while JetBlue’s Inflight Crewmembers remained covered by a separate TWU agreement.
  • United CWA-AFA communications described its own contract implementation, CQ training rules and October scheduling process, supporting continued carrier-specific labor administration.
  • No public combined-carrier Flight Attendant application, joint CWA-AFA/TWU representation protocol, or common crew-scheduling arrangement was identified in the sources reviewed through the cutoff.
  • No dated completion notice for a single itinerary combining both carriers’ flights was verified by this cutoff.
  • The October 8 notice described benefits-volunteer training during the preceding week on United’s 2026–2031 agreement.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Look for evidence of shared management or control, centralized labor and personnel functions, common scheduling or qualification systems, mixed crews, or transfers of work across the two Flight Attendant groups.
  • Distinguish new customer-sales or loyalty features from changes to employment, operating authority and labor administration.
  • Check any NMB application and subsequent action on their own terms; a commercial alliance does not automatically produce a representation vote, and a formal corporate-merger label is not a prerequisite to an NMB inquiry.
  • Track the announced JFK access, mixed-carrier itinerary capability, and remaining commercial phases as future milestones until dated sources confirm completion; do not treat planned customer features as labor integration.

Under Sections 19.4, 19.601 and 19.7 of the NMB Representation Manual, an organization or individual may apply with evidence of representation or the required showing of employee support. Incumbents may provide existing representation records; applicants representing none of the affected employees must submit valid authorization cards. A single-system finding leads to further investigation of representation, not an automatic election. Existing certifications remain effective until the Board issues a new certification or dismissal.
