October 22, 2026. This guide connects the United/CWA-AFA 2026–2031 CBA and the JetBlue/TWU agreement, as amended, to the detailed United/JetBlue comparison. The linked analysis separates existing contractual terms, implementation-dependent changes and proposed provisions for a hypothetical combined agreement. [S49] [S20] [S37]

United and JetBlue remain separate employers for this comparison. A commercial partnership does not itself replace their contracts, resolve representation or create an integrated seniority list. The Blue Sky Single-Carrier Watch addresses the evidence relevant to an NMB inquiry. [S13]

Compare the benefit, the condition and the date

A higher hourly rate is not necessarily a higher payment for every assignment. A more detailed clause is not automatically a stronger protection in every transaction. A future improvement is not yet a current operating rule.

The comparison therefore pairs each material benefit with its unit, eligibility, effective date and exceptions. Proposed combined-contract terms are evaluated as negotiated design choices, not rights that would arise automatically from an acquisition or representation result.

The NMB representation process, seniority integration and joint-contract bargaining answer different questions. Selection of a representative does not itself select a wage scale, assign a final seniority position or eliminate an existing contract. Scope and protection during a transaction

JetBlue Article 3 distinguishes acquiring another company from being acquired. The provisions must follow the assumed transaction, rather than treating the acquiring-carrier timetable as a universal rule. United’s Section 1 and its separate no-furlough LOA likewise require attention to the protection’s trigger and covered employees. [S20] [S18] [S26]

Enforcement and access to information

Access to assignment records, scheduling systems and payroll evidence can help identify a breach. Grievance deadlines, available remedies and arbitration procedures determine what happens next. Neither transparency alone nor a formal procedure alone establishes that a particular dispute will be resolved quickly.

The combined-contract comparison considers both practical evidence access and enforceable relief, including the distinct procedures attached to scope and successorship disputes. Read the enforcement and information-rights comparison →

Governance and employee choice

Union structure and a contract’s observed provisions are different subjects. A carrier-specific Local, local councils and a master executive council allocate authority in different ways; those structures alone do not prove bargaining effectiveness, resource dilution or a future representation result.

A combined contract is a negotiated package

The proposed model preserves strong pay floors and meaningful work protections while identifying valuable provisions from each agreement. It does not assume automatic absorption of the smaller group, promise every favorable feature without offsets, or treat incompatible formulas as cumulative.

Coverage, payment units, benefit eligibility, implementation obligations and enforcement would need to work together. The linked matrices explain the tradeoffs and identify where a proposed protection would go beyond the existing agreements.
