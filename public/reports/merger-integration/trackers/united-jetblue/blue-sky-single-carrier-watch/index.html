Blue Sky Single-Carrier Watch

Where It Stands and What Could Trigger a Flight Attendant Representation Vote

Method: This series measures Blue Sky against the National Mediation Board’s published representation procedures and against the documented Alaska / Hawaiian, Allegiant / Sun Country, and Republic / Mesa examples. CrewSignal distinguishes confirmed facts, announced future plans, and analytical inferences. It does not assume that a commercial partnership, common marketing, or reciprocal customer benefits automatically establish a single transportation system.

Current Assessment

Based on the public record reviewed through September 18, 2026, Blue Sky shows meaningful customer-facing coordination but does not yet present the combination of common ownership, common management, centralized labor relations, integrated essential operations, or a combined Flight Attendant workforce that would support describing United and JetBlue as a single transportation system.

JetBlue says the airlines are not merging and will remain separate. Customers can earn and redeem loyalty currency and purchase eligible flights operated by either carrier through either airline’s channels, but mixed-carrier connecting journeys still require separate itineraries, the partnership is not a codeshare, and the operating carrier remains identified.

That assessment can change as facts change. CrewSignal will track each public development against the NMB’s published indicators rather than treating the companies’ chosen label as conclusive.

Current Indicator Snapshot

  • Present: cross-carrier sales, reciprocal loyalty earning and redemption, reciprocal premium benefits, and common Blue Sky marketing.
  • Announced for future phases: single itineraries mixing both operating carriers, additional Paisly-powered services, and United access to JetBlue-controlled JFK opportunities.
  • Not publicly established: common ownership, overlapping boards, common executive management, centralized Flight Attendant labor relations, integrated crew scheduling, combined training, mixed Flight Attendant crews, common bases, a combined workforce, or a common Flight Attendant agreement.
  • Representation status: CWA-AFA represents United Flight Attendants, TWU Local 579 represents JetBlue Inflight Crewmembers, and no public joint protocol or NMB application has been identified.

How a Representation Process Could Begin

  • The NMB Manual defines a covered transaction more broadly than a conventional merger label, including a consolidation, purchase, lease, operating contract, acquisition of control, or similar transaction.
  • An organization or individual would have to file an application seeking a determination that a single transportation system exists.
  • The NMB would conduct a fact-specific investigation into integration indicators such as combined routes, uniforms, marketing, scheduling or dispatching, labor and personnel operations, management, workforce, and ownership.
  • A single-system finding would move the matter into the representation phase; it would not automatically create a vote or select a representative.
  • Each applicant would then need evidence covering at least 50 percent of the combined craft or class. Incumbents may use current-representation records, while a filer representing none of the employees must use valid authorization cards as its showing of interest.
  • If a representation dispute remains, the NMB may use a secret ballot or another appropriate method. The Board controls eligibility, ballot procedures, and the ultimate certification or dismissal.

What the Other Trackers Show

  • Alaska / Hawaiian shows a same-union path: CWA-AFA represented both legacy groups, obtained a combined certification, and avoided a contested representation election, while the difficult work of a JCBA, seniority integration, and legacy-contract enforcement continued.
  • Allegiant / Sun Country shows common ownership and a declared integration path with two incumbent Flight Attendant unions, but no public transition protocol, application, combined certification, or seniority process.
  • Republic / Mesa shows an actual single-system determination based on substantial managerial, labor, personnel, contract, seniority, policy, and operating-certificate integration even though every public-facing indicator was not present.
  • Blue Sky currently presents a materially earlier and more customer-facing set of facts than those three examples.
