Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.10.16

Editor’s Note: CrewSignal uses public records to compare how unions structure, negotiate, communicate, and protect Flight Attendants during mergers and representation proceedings. Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Allegiant / Sun Country Flight Attendant integration or representation record was identified for October 10–16, 2026. Public sources do not provide a complete account of private labor activity.

The acquisition closed May 13, 2026. In its August 10 Form 10-Q, Allegiant reported that the airlines continued to operate separately under FAA rules, had applied for a single operating certificate and expected to obtain it in 2028. That is management’s forecast, not a completed FAA milestone.

Dated legacy records identify TWU Local 577 for Allegiant Flight Attendants and IBT Local 120 for Sun Country Flight Attendants. No public Flight Attendant joint-union transition protocol, combined JCBA, implemented joint seniority list or new NMB disposition was located for this week. The legacy provisions described below are not a newly announced cross-carrier agreement.

Integration Dashboard

  • Corporate closing is complete. The company’s May closing announcement said existing collective bargaining agreements would remain in place during integration.
  • The August filing described separate FAA operations and unfinished labor integration. Its anticipated 2028 certificate date remains a company expectation in the verified carry-forward record.
  • Allegiant expected necessary joint-contract negotiations would likely begin after certification of a single post-merger representative. That statement describes management’s anticipated sequence, not a universal legal prerequisite imposed by the NMB.
  • The legacy Allegiant CBA contains acquisition-specific seniority provisions and protection against merger-related involuntary furlough for Allegiant-list Flight Attendants in Section 5.H.3, subject to Section 5.H.4’s scope qualification. This is not evidence of a newly agreed TWU/IBT protocol, a completed seniority integration or equivalent protection covering both groups.
  • The reviewed public record did not establish a new joint arrangement governing mixed crews, work transfers, bases, training or benefits during October 10–16. That finding does not negate the parties’ existing contractual rights.
  • Common ownership, FAA certification, an NMB single-system determination and the Board’s representation disposition are separate questions. An application or single-system finding does not automatically require a ballot.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Will the companies publish a dated FAA integration update that confirms or revises the 2028 certificate expectation?
  • Will TWU Local 577 and IBT Local 120 publish a Flight Attendant transition protocol, representation strategy or identifiable application, and explain member participation?
  • What additional shared protections will address work transfers, mixed crews, bases, training, benefits and furlough exposure during separate operations?
  • How will the legacy seniority provisions be implemented, and when will the parties document a combined list, joint-contract process and effective common work rules?
  • What evidence will show actual integration of Flight Attendant management, labor administration, scheduling and qualifications, separately from common ownership or customer-facing changes?
