Integration Tracker

Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.11.20

Status Summary

Allegiant announced plans on November 17 for 16 routes across Allegiant and Sun Country for spring and summer 2027. The announcement included planned changes in which airline will fly some routes. It said the airlines would continue operating separately until 2028 or later, with their own policies and operations.

The route plans do not show that the flights have started or that crews will work under shared rules. No new Flight Attendant representation, joint-contract, or seniority implementation milestone was identified in the public sources reviewed.

Integration Dashboard

  • Ownership: Allegiant completed its acquisition of Sun Country on May 13. The November 17 route announcement describes continued separate operations and does not report a new Flight Attendant integration milestone.
  • Operating integration: Allegiant’s November 6 filing reported that it had applied for a single Federal Aviation Administration operating certificate and expected both the certificate and a combined passenger reservation system in 2028. Those are separate milestones and neither forecast establishes completion.
  • Flight Attendant integration: The public sources reviewed did not identify a new representation determination, joint Flight Attendant agreement, or evidence of combined bidding, assignments, or training.
  • Next disclosure: Allegiant previously announced a December 7 Investor Day. Any integration update from that event was still ahead.

  • What Flight Attendant agreement or representation record will explain how the two groups move toward shared contract terms?
  • What shows that an integrated seniority list actually determines bids, bases, or assignments?
