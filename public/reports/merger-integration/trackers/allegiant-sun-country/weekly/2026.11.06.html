Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.11.06

Historical window: October 31–November 6, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Cutoff: November 7, 2026, 07:59:59 UTC.

Status Summary

Allegiant’s November 4 earnings release and its Form 10-Q dated November 6 provide a current corporate and operational update. Allegiant reaffirmed the May 13 acquisition closing and reported continuing integration work. Neither document establishes a completed Flight Attendant representation or joint-contract milestone.

The 10-Q says the airlines continue operating separately under FAA rules. Management currently expects a single operating certificate in 2028. This is an expectation, not FAA issuance.

Integration Dashboard

  • Corporate acquisition: closed May 13, 2026; this is carry-forward context reaffirmed by the week’s release.
  • FAA and customer systems: the company reports an SOC application and work toward one passenger reservation system, with combined PSS implementation expected in 2028. PSS integration does not establish integrated crew scheduling or qualifications.
  • Labor: management links combining operations to SOC issuance and joint collective bargaining agreements. Its statement that joint negotiations may be needed, likely following certification of a single post-merger representative where necessary, describes a prospective sequence, not an NMB decision or completed Flight Attendant agreement.
  • Training: Allegiant’s November 2 announcement describes a two-day UNLV customer-experience program for Allegiant Flight Attendants, with rollout scheduled from November through spring 2027. It does not establish combined Allegiant/Sun Country qualifications or common work rules.
  • Flight Attendant representation, seniority and common work rules: no dated in-window milestone was established in the bounded public review. TWU Local 577’s latest announcements require member access; the public Local 120 news index supplied no relevant in-window item. This is not evidence that private transition work stopped.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Look for dated FAA confirmation, revised SOC forecasts, and a completed PSS implementation announcement; keep each milestone separate.
  • Look for a Flight Attendant-specific NMB application or determination, union transition agreement, joint bargaining notice, integrated seniority implementation, or documented common rules.
  • Track whether the announced Allegiant training rollout occurs and whether any later notice expressly extends its scope to Sun Country crews. Do not equate customer-service instruction with combined operational qualification.
  • The December 7 Investor Day was future at cutoff. Any subsequent disclosure requires its own publication-date check and is not part of this week’s established record.
