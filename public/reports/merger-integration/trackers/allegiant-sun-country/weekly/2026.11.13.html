Integration Tracker

Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.11.13

Status Summary

No new Flight Attendant representation, joint-contract, seniority or common-work-rule milestone was identified in the public records reviewed. Allegiant’s acquisition of Sun Country closed on May 13. Corporate ownership and completed crew integration remain distinct.

The operational position described in Allegiant’s November 6 Form 10-Q remains the latest dated primary account located: the airlines operate separately under Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) rules, with a single operating certificate (SOC) and combined passenger reservation system (PSS) expected in 2028. These are management forecasts, not completed approvals or implementations.

Integration Dashboard

  • Operating integration: the company reported applying for an SOC and said its ability to combine operations remains limited until the certificate and joint collective bargaining agreements are in place.
  • Flight Attendant representation and bargaining: no new carrier-specific National Mediation Board (NMB) determination or public notice establishing a joint agreement was identified. This does not establish that no application, negotiations or private transition work exists.
  • Seniority and work rules: no dated public evidence located establishes new combined bidding, assignments, reserve rules or seniority-list use.
  • Training: the November 2 University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) announcement schedules customer-experience instruction for Allegiant Flight Attendants from November through spring 2027. It is not evidence of combined Allegiant/Sun Country qualification, and no new dated confirmation of combined-crew delivery was located.
  • Next corporate disclosure: Allegiant’s November 4 release announced a December 7 Investor Day. Its integration discussion remains prospective.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • When will FAA approval and passenger-system implementation be documented separately from their projected dates?
  • Will a Flight Attendant-specific NMB filing or determination, joint bargaining notice, or transition agreement clarify representation and contract integration?
  • What evidence will show an integrated seniority list actually governing bids, bases or assignments, rather than merely being prepared?
  • Will any training notice explicitly cover both Flight Attendant groups and identify operational qualifications or common rules, rather than customer-service instruction alone?
