Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Allegiant / Sun Country Flight Attendant integration or representation record was identified for October 24–30, 2026. The bounded public review does not establish that private bargaining or transition work was inactive.

The acquisition closed May 13, 2026. Allegiant’s August 10 Form 10-Q remains the dated operational baseline: the airlines operated separately under FAA rules, an application for a single operating certificate had been submitted, and management expected issuance in 2028. That forecast is not a completed FAA milestone.

Integration Dashboard

  • At closing, the company said existing collective bargaining agreements would remain in place. The August filing linked the ability to combine operations to obtaining a single operating certificate and joint agreements for unionized workgroups.
  • No new dated Flight Attendant joint contract, combined seniority-list implementation, or shared arrangement for mixed crews, work transfers, bases, training, reserve, scheduling or benefits was established in this review.
  • Corporate ownership and NMB representation are separate questions. Under the NMB’s procedures, carrier notice, a representation application, a single-system finding and the subsequent representation disposition are distinct steps; a finding does not itself make an election inevitable.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Will a dated FAA or company update confirm progress beyond the application stage and preserve or revise the 2028 certificate expectation?
  • Will TWU Local 577 and Teamsters Local 120 publish a Flight Attendant transition protocol, representation strategy or identifiable NMB application?
  • What dated provisions will establish seniority integration and common work rules, including mixed crews, work transfers, bases, training, benefits and furlough protections?
