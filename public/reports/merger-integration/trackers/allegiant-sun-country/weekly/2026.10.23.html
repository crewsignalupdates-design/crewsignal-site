Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.10.23

Editor’s Note: CrewSignal uses public records to compare how unions structure, negotiate, communicate, and protect Flight Attendants during mergers and representation proceedings. Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Allegiant / Sun Country Flight Attendant integration or representation record was identified for October 17–23, 2026. Public sources do not provide a complete account of private labor activity.

The acquisition closed May 13. Allegiant’s August 10 Form 10-Q described the airlines as operating separately under FAA rules, with a single operating certificate application submitted and issuance expected in 2028. The application and management’s forecast are not a completed FAA milestone.

Dated legacy sources identify TWU Local 577 for Allegiant Flight Attendants and Teamsters Local 120 for Sun Country Flight Attendants. This week’s review did not establish a new combined representation disposition, joint contract or implemented combined seniority list.

Integration Dashboard

  • Corporate closing and operational integration remain distinct milestones. At closing, the company said existing collective bargaining agreements would remain in place.
  • The August filing anticipated that necessary joint-contract talks would likely follow certification of a single post-merger representative. This was management’s expected sequence, not a universal legal prerequisite.
  • Section 5.H.3 of Allegiant’s legacy agreement addresses seniority integration and protects Allegiant-list Flight Attendants from involuntary furlough directly resulting from an acquisition, subject to Section 5.H.4’s qualification for acquisitions that do not integrate Flight Attendants. Those provisions do not establish equivalent protection for both groups or prove that integration has occurred.
  • No new public joint arrangement governing mixed crews, work transfers, bases, training, scheduling, reserve or benefits was established in the bounded weekly review. That does not negate existing contractual rights or establish that no private discussions occurred.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • When will a dated FAA or company update confirm progress beyond the application stage, and will it preserve or revise the 2028 certificate expectation?
  • Will TWU Local 577 and Teamsters Local 120 publish a Flight Attendant transition protocol, representation strategy or identifiable NMB application, including how members will participate?
  • What shared protections will address work transfers, mixed crews, bases, training, benefits and furlough exposure while the operations remain separate?
  • How will the parties document seniority integration, a joint-contract process and effective common work rules, rather than treating common ownership as their substitute?
