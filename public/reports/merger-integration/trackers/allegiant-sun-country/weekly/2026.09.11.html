Integration Dashboard

  • Allegiant Air and Sun Country continued to operate separately under FAA rules.
  • The company’s public baseline continued to place the single operating certificate in 2028 and required JCBAs before full operational combination.
  • Allegiant Flight Attendants remained represented by TWU Local 577, while Sun Country Flight Attendants remained represented by IBT Local 120.
  • No public TWU–IBT Flight Attendant transition protocol or NMB application was identified.
  • The unrelated JSX application demonstrated that a representation docket begins with an application rather than with a merger announcement.
  • The NMB Manual permits any organization or individual to apply for a single-transportation-system determination.
  • The Manual’s transaction definition extends beyond arrangements formally titled mergers.
  • A carrier’s transaction notice does not substitute for an employee- or representative-initiated application.
  • Filing begins the investigation but does not automatically establish a single system or authorize an election.
  • The NMB evaluates actual operational, managerial, labor, personnel, workforce, branding, and ownership integration case by case.
  • After a single-system finding, each applicant must establish at least 50 percent support across the combined Flight Attendant craft or class.
  • Incumbents may use current certifications, agreements, seniority lists, dues records, or similar evidence, while a nonincumbent must use valid authorization cards.
  • Applicants, incumbents, and intervenors may supplement their combined-craft showing within 30 calendar days after the determination.
  • A vote may follow if the NMB determines that an election is required, but an election is not the automatic consequence of filing.
  • Existing certifications remain effective until the NMB issues a new certification or dismissal.
