Allegiant / Sun Country

Week ending 2026.10.02

Status Summary

No new notable public reference changing the Allegiant / Sun Country Flight Attendant integration or representation record was identified for September 26–October 2, 2026.

The acquisition closed May 13, 2026. In its August 10 Form 10-Q, Allegiant described the airlines as operating separately under FAA rules, with a single-operating-certificate application submitted and necessary labor integration unfinished. Corporate ownership and an FAA integration plan do not establish a completed NMB representation process.

The verified legacy representation record identifies TWU Local 577 for Allegiant Flight Attendants and IBT Local 120 for Sun Country Flight Attendants. A public Flight Attendant joint-union transition protocol, combined seniority process or new NMB application or disposition was not located for this reporting window. No public record of such activity was identified; private activity may not be publicly disclosed.

Integration Dashboard

  • Allegiant’s May acquisition is a completed ownership event, not a pending transaction.
  • The August SEC filing described separate FAA operations and constraints on full operational combination.
  • TWU Local 577 and IBT Local 120 are distinct incumbent representatives for the two legacy Flight Attendant groups.
  • Allegiant expected that, where necessary, joint-contract negotiations would likely begin after the NMB certified a single post-merger representative. This was management’s anticipated sequence, not an NMB ruling establishing a required bargaining timetable.
  • The public material located does not establish a combined Flight Attendant JCBA, implemented joint seniority list or agreed interim work-transfer protections.
  • An NMB application, single-system finding and representation outcome remain separate procedural events; a vote is not automatic.

CrewSignal Watch Points

  • Watch for evidence that advances or revises the company’s August expectation of a single operating certificate in 2028; that forecast is not an FAA guarantee.
  • Watch whether TWU Local 577 and IBT Local 120 publish a joint arrangement, file separately or otherwise explain their representation strategy and member-participation process.
  • Watch for dated interim protections covering scope, mixed crews, aircraft or work transfers, scheduling, training, bases, benefits and furloughs.
  • Watch for an identifiable NMB filing, any single-system determination, applicable support and intervention steps, and the Board’s eventual disposition without collapsing those stages into an assumed election.
  • Watch for concrete changes in Flight Attendant management, labor administration, scheduling, training and workforce integration rather than relying only on common ownership or customer-facing changes.
