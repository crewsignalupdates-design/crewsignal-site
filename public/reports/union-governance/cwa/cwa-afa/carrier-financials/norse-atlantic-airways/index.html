CWA-AFA · Carrier Financials

Norse Atlantic Airways

Tracing money from parent CWA to the CWA-AFA sector to the visible Norse carrier and local layers.

Quick Read

  • Reported recipients: CWA-AFA’s sector LM-2 identifies funding to both Norse Local Council 22001 and the Norse MEC in FY2024 and FY2026.
  • FY2024: the local received $10,363 and the MEC $8,888. The local accounted for 53.8% of those two reported amounts.
  • FY2026: the local received $19,306 and the MEC $23,028. The local accounted for 45.6% of those two reported amounts.
  • Reporting periods: FY2024 ended May 31, 2024, and FY2026 ended May 31, 2026. Neither filing identifies spending in response to the November 2026 suspension.

Filing Map

Parent CWA, the CWA-AFA sector and airline councils are distinct reporting levels. The amounts below are sector disbursements to named affiliates in Schedule 15, Representational Activities. A sector payment to a council does not establish that council’s complete receipts, expenditures or remaining balance.

  • Parent CWA filings: FY ending 05/31/2025; FY ending 05/31/2024; FY ending 05/31/2023.
  • CWA-AFA sector filings: FY ending 05/31/2026; FY ending 05/31/2025; FY ending 05/31/2024; FY ending 05/31/2023.

Sector transfers and council accounts

The sector filing names the recipients as “AFA LEC 22001 NORSE ATLANTIC AIRWAYS” and “AFA MEC NORSE ATLANTIC AIRWAYS.” These are payments reported by the sector, not standalone annual reports filed by either council.

A council’s own filing and accounts would be needed to establish its other income, expenditure and balance. The absence of a separate council return from these links does not establish that no return exists or that the council received no funding.

Reported CWA-AFA sector funding

  • FY2026: Local Council 22001 $19,306; Norse MEC $23,028; combined subtotal $42,334. FY2026 LM-2, Schedule 15.
  • FY2025: the sector reports $30,617 for the Norse MEC. That amount alone does not establish the local’s funding or share. FY2025 LM-2, Schedule 15.
  • FY2024: Local Council 22001 $10,363; Norse MEC $8,888; combined subtotal $19,251. FY2024 LM-2, Schedule 15.

The FY2024 and FY2026 entries identify both local and MEC recipients. Each subtotal above adds only those two identified payments; it is not a measure of all resources supporting Norse flight attendants.

Local share of the two reported amounts

For each year with both amounts identified, the local share is the payment to Local Council 22001 divided by the combined payments to that local and the Norse MEC.

Formula: local amount ÷ (local amount + MEC amount) × 100.

  • FY2026: $19,306 ÷ ($19,306 + $23,028) × 100 = 45.6%.
  • FY2024: $10,363 ÷ ($10,363 + $8,888) × 100 = 53.8%.
  • FY2025: the MEC amount alone cannot establish a local percentage; a missing local amount must not be treated as zero.

These percentages describe the split between the two identified sector payments. They are not shares of all Norse-related spending, measures of services delivered, or evidence of amounts spent by parent CWA.

Representation and Bargaining Visibility

Norse has a visible local structure through Council 01 FLL/JFK on the elections site. Norse also has a visible bargaining timeline: the parties reached a tentative agreement in April 2025 and ratified it in May 2025.

For a deeper explanation of how CWA-AFA negotiating committees are chosen, how staff negotiators are assigned, and where compensation appears in public reporting, see How CWA-AFA Negotiating Committees and Staff Negotiators Work .

Historical funding and the November suspension

The FY2024 and FY2026 reporting years ended on May 31 of their respective years. Both precede the November 2026 flight suspension. Neither filing establishes what CWA or CWA-AFA spent responding to that event. Crisis assistance requires its own evidence of authorization, expenditure and delivery. See Union Services in an Airline Crisis for the documented response and funding channels.
