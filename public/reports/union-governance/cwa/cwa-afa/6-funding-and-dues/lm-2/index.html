What the evidence says about parent services

SIF 726 supplies a specific answer to the service question. CWA’s 2025 Defense Fund Oversight Committee report identifies a funded corporate campaign researcher supporting CWA-AFA bargaining and organizing. It describes work concerning United, Delta and regional boarding pay. Its assessment of bargaining impact is the union’s own; the public summary gives no project budget, actual cost, named researcher, hours or reimbursement ledger.

Separately, parent FY2024 Schedule 12 reports Tiffany Loftin, CWA-AFA Senior Campaign Lead, with $131,191 gross salary and $14,977 official-business disbursements: $146,168 in total. The row is entirely allocated to representation. It does not establish a link to SIF 726 or that the parent ultimately absorbed the whole cost. The saved parent print stops inside Schedule 15; this staff row is fully visible.

Shared services also have a cost side. The 2023 CWA Finance Committee described an agreement with CWA-AFA that increased the parent’s FY2023–24 budget other income by $1 million for shared services. This is budgeted parent revenue, not a reconciled receipt. The 2024 committee then described reviewing shared expenditures with sector Secretary-Treasurer Dante Harris.

The parent’s $1 million shared-services budget addition and the sector’s separate $1 million of CWA contribution receipts run in opposite directions and have different descriptions. Matching amounts do not establish the same transaction, an offset or net merger value.
