A verified receipt and a documented service relationship

The preserved FY2024 CWA-AFA sector LM-2 records a $55,002 expense reimbursement from APFA. APFA also publicly described receiving bargaining and organizing assistance from CWA-AFA in 2023. The available records establish a financial relationship and reported services, but do not connect that payment to a particular assignment or invoice.

Sources: FY2024 sector LM-2, Schedule 14, PDF page 41; APFA statement, September 26, 2023.

What APFA said it received

In its September 26, 2023 statement, APFA said CWA-AFA had made Joe Burns available as APFA’s lead negotiator and strategy advisor. APFA identified his CWA-AFA role at that time as Director of Collective Bargaining and General Counsel, and also reported organizing and media training from the sector. This is APFA’s account of the assistance it received.

Sources: APFA, “Solidarity: Doing Things Differently,” September 26, 2023.

The statement supplies useful service context. It does not identify which services the $55,002 receipt reimbursed, the total cost of the assignments, or any parent CWA contribution. The payment is an APFA-to-sector receipt; it cannot by itself answer how much the 2004-era merger benefited CWA-AFA.

Records needed to follow the payment

  • Match both sides: reconcile the sector receipt with APFA’s payment entry, including date, amount, counterparty and any timing difference between fiscal periods.
  • Identify the work: obtain the agreement, invoices and project codes showing whether reimbursement covered bargaining, legal work, training, travel or another service.
  • Identify the cost and payer: distinguish sector employees, parent CWA personnel and outside firms; record staff time, benefits and expenses, and who initially paid each cost.
  • Determine the balance: compare reimbursable costs with amounts actually recovered before describing the arrangement as a subsidy, a profit or full cost recovery.
  • Complete the annual record: recover the FY2023 and FY2025 sector bodies and reciprocal APFA records before certifying the older amounts or a trend.

These are outstanding reconciliation questions as of the review cutoff. A receipt labeled reimbursement does not disclose the underlying service allocation. The APFA financial review provides the related union-level financial context.
