Parent services and unresolved cost attribution

SIF 726 supplies a specific answer to the service question. CWA’s 2025 Defense Fund Oversight Committee report identifies a funded corporate campaign researcher supporting CWA-AFA bargaining and organizing. It describes work concerning United, Delta and regional boarding pay. Its assessment of bargaining impact is the union’s own; the public summary gives no project budget, actual cost, named researcher, hours or reimbursement ledger.

Separately, parent FY2024 Schedule 12 reports Tiffany Loftin, CWA-AFA Senior Campaign Lead, with $131,191 gross salary and $14,977 official-business disbursements: $146,168 in total. The row is entirely allocated to representation. It does not establish a link to SIF 726 or that the parent ultimately absorbed the whole cost. The saved parent print stops inside Schedule 15; this staff row is fully visible.

Shared services also have a cost side. The 2023 CWA Finance Committee described an agreement with CWA-AFA that increased the parent’s FY2023–24 budget other income by $1 million for shared services. This is budgeted parent revenue, not a reconciled receipt. The 2024 committee then described reviewing shared expenditures with sector Secretary-Treasurer Dante Harris.

The parent’s $1 million shared-services budget addition and the sector’s separate $1 million of CWA contribution receipts run in opposite directions and have different descriptions. Matching amounts do not establish the same transaction, an offset or net merger value.

Sources: 2025 DFOC report, pp4 and 8; parent FY2024 Schedule 12, p50; 2023 proceedings, p118; 2024 Finance Committee, p2.

Salary, lost time and release-time reporting

A salary line is one component of compensation reporting. Union-paid lost time, employer payments later reimbursed by a union, allowances and expenses need to be distinguished. A zero salary line does not establish the absence of all economic support.

For the LM-2 schedules used in these historical reports, DOL places officers’ lost-time payments in Schedule 11, Column D, and employee lost-time payments in Schedule 12, Column D. DOL also discusses employer payment followed by union reimbursement.

Employer-paid leave requires the agreement, payroll and reimbursement records to determine the economic payer. The union return alone does not quantify every arrangement. Retained United material includes REIMBURSE LOCAL FOR FPL WAGES and FPL ADMIN FEES; those labels or a low salary do not establish a particular leave arrangement.

Sources: DOL: Union Lost Time Payments, reporting instructions; DOL October 2004 LM-2 FAQ: employer payment and reimbursement; dated United entry.

Interpreting the retained FY2025 financing and asset figures

Retained FY2025 figures require financing and capital context. Total cash receipts of $39,463,382 include $9,750,000 of loans; total disbursements of $39,032,554 include $10,792,425 of investment/fixed-asset purchases. Their $430,828 difference is a cash-flow measure. It cannot be treated as operating profit or equivalent recurring revenue growth.

On those retained figures, $9,750,000 borrowed less $37,005 repaid equals $9,712,995 closing loans. That arithmetic is consistent, but the original loan and asset schedules remain outstanding.

The listed cash, Treasury securities, investments and other assets total $16,057,935, leaving $9,832,845 of the displayed $25,890,780 total outside the selected breakdown. The original statement must identify the omitted category. The residual is not a verified fixed-asset or property value, and no filing error is inferred.

Sources: FY2025 official link — original body not recovered; retained annual-history CSV.

Records that can resolve the merger-service question

  • SIF 726: obtain the authorization, account code, budget, actual ledger, staff/vendor allocations, reimbursement, deliverables and evaluations. DFOC directs members seeking an SIF report to the Secretary-Treasurer’s office.
  • Shared services: obtain the executed agreement, amendments, department/service schedule, allocation formula, invoices and reciprocal settlements.
  • Specialist work: identify the matter, department/provider, dates, nonprivileged work description, billed/allocated cost and ultimate payer. Examine payment metadata without disclosing confidential legal advice.
  • Reserves and consolidated accounts: reconcile ownership, withdrawal restrictions, investment earnings, fees and elimination entries before treating a transfer as a cost or benefit.
  • 2026 execution: compare the revised budget, rebate records and actual surplus use with the adopted authority; seek corrected reports and accounting-system reconciliations.

Sources: SIF report-access route, p4; shared-services agreement reference, p118; adopted budget action.
