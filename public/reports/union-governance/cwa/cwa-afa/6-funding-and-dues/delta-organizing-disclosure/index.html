A documented campaign history with a specific funding lead

Primary records establish several distinct Delta organizing episodes. The strongest additional financial lead is CWA’s SIF 726 research project, which expressly included Delta organizing. The reviewed records do not supply a complete Delta-specific expenditure total. Sector-wide spending and membership figures cannot fill that gap.

What CWA’s fund report adds

The 2025 Defense Fund Oversight Committee report says SIF 726 supplied a corporate campaign researcher whose work included Delta organizing, United bargaining and regional boarding pay. This is affirmative evidence of CWA-funded support. The narrative does not disclose the project’s expenditure or Delta allocation, so the whole project cannot be assigned to Delta.

Sources: 2025 Defense Fund Oversight Committee report, PDF page 8.

The FY2024 United observation and FY2025 sector membership total describe different reporting dates. They do not establish a United funding percentage. Even membership counts for the same period would require actual dues, remittance and service-allocation records before they could support an attribution of Delta costs to United members.

What would produce a defensible Delta cost total?

  • Recover the complete annual sector and parent filing bodies and project records for the relevant periods, including FY2023 onward.
  • Obtain SIF 726’s funding authorization, actual spending and allocation of researcher time among Delta, United and regional work.
  • Identify Delta-coded staff time, counsel, research, travel, communications and organizing invoices, including costs paid outside the sector.
  • Reconcile coalition cost sharing and reimbursements so transfers are not counted a second time as new campaign spending.
  • Separate the resulting cost record from any claim about election outcomes, bargaining gains or the net benefit of the CWA merger.

As of the review cutoff, the linked records establish activity and a specific parent-funding lead. The missing allocation records prevent a complete cost calculation; they do not establish zero support.
