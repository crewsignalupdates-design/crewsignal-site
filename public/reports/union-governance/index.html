Union Governance

Union Governance & Representation

Unions are in the business of representation. CrewSignal examines what members receive for their dues, who controls decisions and spending, and how organizational structure affects the quality, accessibility, and accountability of services at the local level.

This section is for informational purposes only and summarizes publicly available governance documents at a structural level. It does not provide legal advice, member guidance, or individualized recommendations.

What this analysis measures

Members finance the union and have a stake in how it operates. We assess financial stewardship, access to representation, bargaining and enforcement support, the responsiveness of local services, and members' ability to influence decisions and hold leaders accountable. Where the evidence permits, we connect resources and decisions to services delivered and documented outcomes. Where records are incomplete, we identify what cannot yet be determined.

Six common areas of analysis

  • Overview & Governing Documents: What is the organization, which rules control it, and which version is current?
  • Governance Structure & Decision Authority: Who can authorize, fund, delay, override, or review action?
  • Representation & Member Services: Who provides services, to whom, through what access and escalation routes?
  • Elections, Terms & Member Control: How can members select, challenge, and hold decision-makers accountable?
  • Contract Administration, Enforcement & Committees: How are bargaining, grievances, arbitration, and specialist support delivered?
  • Financial Transparency & LM Reporting: How much is collected, retained, transferred, spent, and supported by verifiable records?
