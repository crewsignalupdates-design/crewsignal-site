The denominator changes the meaning
Ratio Result
$8,717,630 representation ÷ $14,012,937 total disbursements 62.2%
$8,717,630 representation ÷ $13,009,224 dues and agency fees 67.0%

The first percentage describes the share of union-wide disbursements classified as representation. The second compares that spending with dues receipts. Neither isolates local spending, a per-base budget or service effectiveness. The amount already spent is not a maximum pool available for future work.

Allocated compensation describes how disbursements were reported. It does not establish where a staff member worked, which base received a service, how many cases were handled or what result followed. Those questions require operational records beyond this schedule.

The $1,473,992 decrease in net assets equals the $1,215,224 decrease in assets plus the $258,768 increase in liabilities. This identifies where the reported change appears. It does not establish the cause of the investment or payable movement, and it is not explained by saying that almost all annual receipts were deployed.

Cash, investments and fixed assets have different uses. The $14,875,873 investment balance is not automatically unrestricted liquid cash available for operations. Underlying fund restrictions, investment movements and audited notes are needed before making stronger reserve or liquidity claims. The filing reports no loan or mortgage balances, but it does report $2,075,570 of accounts payable.

Sources: Statement A, PDF page 3; Schedule 5 investments, PDF page 9; Schedule 8 payables, PDF page 12.

Historical filing fields and current governance

The FY2024 filing reports outside audit or review, $500,000 fidelity bond coverage, and no contingent liabilities or pledged assets. These are the filing’s responses; the actual auditor’s report was not acquired as part of this review.

Its next-regular-election field says February 2028. That is a historical filing field, not the current schedule. APFA’s current Constitution provides three-year national-officer terms, and the corrected October 14, 2026 election notice covers the 2027 national and base elections.

Sources: Items 10–21, PDF page 2; Article VI, current national-officer terms; Corrected 2027 election notice.

This historical report does not establish separately reportable APFA councils, lodges or districts. Current base representation, budgeting rules and later financial periods are addressed in the linked governance sections.
