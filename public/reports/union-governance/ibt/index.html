International Brotherhood of Teamsters

IBT Governance & Financial Review

Who controls representation, how members obtain help, and what the International and local financial records show about the services financed by their dues.

Reviewed November 11, 2026 (Pacific time). Financial analysis begins with FY2023; earlier records are included where they explain authority, services or opening balances.

Six Areas of Review

  1. Overview & Governing Documents

    The available constitution, later governing instruments and the developments that change the current analysis.

  2. Governance Structure & Decision Authority

    Local, Joint Council, Airline Division and International powers; funding decisions, intervention and independent oversight.

  3. Representation & Member Services

    The carrier/local map, current Republic/Mesa certification, access to assistance and evidence of work delivered.

  4. Elections, Terms & Member Control

    Delegate nominations, direct member voting, the unopposed 2026 outcome and the mechanisms for challenging decisions.

  5. Contract Administration, Enforcement & Committees

    Grievance and arbitration processes, release pay, employer reimbursements and implementation of negotiated terms.

  6. Financial Transparency & LM Reporting

    Parent and local accounts, accounting-scope reconciliations, professional-service spending and the merger reimbursement trail.

Evidence Coverage

The review separates governing authority, available service capacity, reported delivery, measured outcomes and attributable cost. A large treasury or a department description alone cannot establish the value received by a particular flight-attendant group.

The financial comparison includes FY2023–FY2024 consolidated audited statements, a partial amended FY2023 LM-2, and IRS-derived summaries for Locals 120 and 135 through FY2025. The parent’s FY2025 audit contents, complete later LM-2 schedules and carrier-specific service allocations remain open records questions. Qualifications appear beside the affected figures.
