Purpose

CrewSignal's TWU reporting distinguishes International governing documents, carrier-Local rules, collective bargaining agreements, federal representation procedures, historical transaction records, and policy documents that are referenced but not yet publicly recovered. A carrier CBA does not establish an International policy; an International policy does not necessarily supersede carrier-specific contractual protections; and historical merger practice is not automatically a current rule.

Source Status Key

  • Current Control Document: presently governing source for the level described.
  • Carrier-Specific Control: governing only the identified Local, carrier, or bargaining unit.
  • Federal Authority: statute, NMB procedure, or other federal rule.
  • Historical Comparator: evidence of prior policy or practice, not automatically a current rule.
  • Corroborating Reference: establishes that another document or policy exists or was invoked.
  • Unresolved Source: referenced or expected document whose controlling current text has not been authenticated.

TWU Airline Merger Policy: Current Evidentiary Status

Verified: TWU has an Airline Merger Policy, and a current Flight Attendant CBA expressly incorporates it by reference. Historical dispatcher agreements and arbitration records independently corroborate the policy's existence and prior application.

Not yet verified: the currently controlling standalone text, its version or effective date, adoption authority, present scope across Air Division crafts, current seniority standard, member vote rights, representative-selection rules, or arbitration mechanism.

The current FSI agreement is particularly important because it provides present-tense contractual confirmation rather than merely historical reference. Section 3 applies both the CBA and the TWU Airline Merger Policy when the other Flight Attendant group is also TWU-represented, provides that the CBA controls a conflict, and separately assigns representation of an integrated group to NMB rules and procedures.

Source-Control Rule

CrewSignal does not promote a referenced policy into verified current text merely because a CBA, arbitration award, or historical agreement names it. Likewise, historical policy content is dated and attributed rather than silently carried forward into the present.

Financial sources and their periods

The financial source register now identifies complete FY2023–FY2025 Form 990 bodies for the parent and Locals 556, 577 and 579, with each entity’s EIN, year end, accounting basis and exact filing links. These are annual tax-return series; unrecovered annual LM-2 bodies are not filled from memory or inferred from the next year’s opening balance.

The complete parent FY2025 LM-2 is an 87-page preserved copy of report 925280, file 000-218, covering September 1, 2024–August 31, 2025. The 2025 Convention Financial Report covers four years, July 1, 2021–June 30, 2025, with a different balance-sheet date. Each is labeled separately in the analysis.

Local 578 financial return bodies, annual audit bodies, complete amendment histories and transaction-level parent/local reconciliations remain unrecovered or incomplete. Those source gaps do not establish zero activity or noncompliance.

The Winter 2023, 2024 and 2025 TWU Express agency-fee expense statements and accompanying published auditor-report texts are now separately identified in the financial source register. They address a defined expense calculation. The reviewed editions retain percentage, date and cross-document reconciliation questions and do not supply the missing local annual audits.

The IRS Texas registry confirms Local 578’s current EIN as 99-3009278 and its recorded August year-end month. Registry affiliation and requirement codes do not establish annual group-return coverage or financial results.

Current developments and source limits

The 2021 and 2025 Constitution booklets were compared directly. Article IV’s stated vice-president count changes from 15 to 18 and its additional International Executive Board count from 40 to 59. The earlier edition already allowed expansion of vice presidents, and both contain transition language. This document comparison is not an official amendment history or a payroll headcount.

A publicly reproduced September 11, 2026 letter assigns International Vice President Gary Peterson to oversee and assist Local 556 and examine its finances. A September 24 Dallas Morning News report independently corroborates the assignment. The official-host original and subsequent findings remain unrecovered. The letter does not establish formal trusteeship or misconduct.

Local 556’s official news index lists September 21 bylaw, October 15 leadership and November 4 International updates. The November excerpt describes receipt of a preliminary review; the full report and conclusions were not recovered. A secondary-host reproduction of the October statement says Kristina Peterson took leave and Sam Wilkins would perform presidential duties during the absence. It is treated as a reported temporary arrangement; no motive, medical detail, resignation or removal is inferred.

Local 579’s official special-election notice and linked 2026 tally provide current elected-office results. Its full bylaws remain behind member access; election notices and tax-return governance descriptions do not substitute for the controlling text.

The September 11 Local 556 artifact is a letter image reproduced by a secondary publisher; no signature is visible in the recovered image. It is not an authenticated signed original. The October 23 AirWaves audio has been preserved and matched to the public feed, but no transcript or substantive audio review was completed, so it supplies no additional findings here.

Carrier service funding and contract versions

The current FSI agreement is effective January 1, 2026 through January 1, 2031; the main agreement signatures are dated January 7, 2026. Sections 19 and 23 establish arbitration and release-pay cost rules, and Section 3.C continues to incorporate TWU’s merger policy without reproducing it. The former 2021–2026 Contract Architecture analysis and its score are historical and require reassessment against this successor.

The official JetBlue CBA bundle replaces a tentative-agreement-labeled preserved copy as the principal source for the newly reviewed clauses. Its November 2025 upload is not a guarantee that every later letter of agreement is consolidated. The Allegiant CISM compensation MOA documents a negotiated payer change, subject to its stated review and reversion conditions. These contract rules are not proof of actual usage or outcomes.
