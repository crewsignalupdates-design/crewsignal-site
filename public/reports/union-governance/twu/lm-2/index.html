These official TWU publications contain statements of calculation of chargeable and nonchargeable expenses and accompanying text headed “Independent Auditors’ Report.” Each covers a year ending August 31 on a modified-cash basis. The report pages do not show an auditor name, signature or separately dated signature block. They are not the missing local annual financial-statement audits.

Local 578 IRS Registry Record

The preserved Texas EO Business Master File identifies EIN 99-3009278 once, at physical CSV line 153083 including the header. The record was checked against all 28 fields of the supplied excerpt and the January 2026 IRS code guide. It identifies group exemption 0694, subordinate affiliation and August as the year-end month. The tax-period and actual asset, income and revenue fields are blank.

The official IRS index gives October 13, 2026 as the data-posting date; the retained packet records retrieval on November 12. A registry record does not replace an annual return or prove annual group-return coverage.

Records still to recover or reconcile

The parent FY2023 and FY2024 LM-2 bodies were not recovered in this review. Earlier report-ID leads are not substituted for inspected bodies. Local 556 official annual LM bodies and Locals 577/579 standalone LM identities and bodies remain to be recovered or reconfirmed. The new Form 990 series supplies verified annual tax data in the meantime.

Local 578’s IRS registry identity is now confirmed, but the retrieved public tax index contains no annual financial return data. The outstanding records are its OLMS identity, applicable annual returns or verified group-return inclusion, audits and opening balances. An absent retrieval is not a finding of nonfiling.

Annual audit bodies, complete amendment histories, parent/local dues reconciliations, carrier allocations of parent services and current service results remain distinct open records. A financial audit opinion does not itself certify member-service quality.
