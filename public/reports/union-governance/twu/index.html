Transport Workers Union of America

TWU Governance & Representation

TWU is a multi-industry union whose Constitution distributes authority among Local memberships, elected Local governing bodies, International institutions, and industry Divisions administered by Internationally appointed directors. CrewSignal traces those control points through elections, bargaining and ratification, grievance administration, financial governance, and merger and seniority protections—distinguishing constitutional authority, carrier-local practice, contractual protections, and unresolved policy questions.

Methodology: CrewSignal distinguishes verified source material, carrier-specific practice, analytical interpretation, and hypothesis. Organizational structure, treasury size, or resource allocation is not treated by itself as proof of bargaining quality, service quality, or causation.
