Transport Workers Union of America

TWU Governance & Representation

Union governance is also business governance: members fund representation, and leadership is accountable for how those resources are used. CrewSignal evaluates what members receive for their dues, who controls decisions and spending, and how International structure affects service at the carrier and local level.

Reviewed November 12, 2026

Methodology: CrewSignal distinguishes verified source material, carrier-specific practice, analytical interpretation, and hypothesis. Organizational structure, treasury size, or resource allocation is not treated by itself as proof of bargaining quality, service quality, or causation.

What this review adds

The November review adds complete FY2023–FY2025 tax-return series for the International and Locals 556, 577 and 579, a parent-to-local payment trail, and carrier-specific rules for funding representation work. It also identifies changes in the stated International officer structure and carefully dated Local 556 oversight and leadership developments.

The full current Airline Merger Policy, Local 578 annual financial records, detailed allocation of parent services and measurable carrier-level service results remain open evidence questions. Financial capacity, documented spending and delivered results are assessed separately.

Open the financial source register

Additional source review confirms Local 578’s IRS registry identity and adds published parent agency-fee expense statements for 2023–2025. The analysis separates their purpose from local annual audits, records unresolved numerical and document-version differences, and identifies the monthly release-time reports that could help trace service costs.
