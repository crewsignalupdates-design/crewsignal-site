Worked example, not a scorecard. This tests a proposed comparison method; it does not adopt a numerical scoring system or change existing CrewSignal scores.

For the synthetic two-day pairing defined below, a Flight Attendant with 5.5 completed pay-service years has modeled pairing wage values of approximately $725.29 at United, $692.16 at Alaska, $673.40 at Southwest under the conditional reporting model, and approximately $719.47 at American. The differences come from dated wage rates, the minimum-pay provision that wins under each agreement, and separately payable boarding compensation—not from matching similarly numbered pay-table rows or imposing a universal conversion between hourly pay and Trips for Pay (TFP). 1, 2, 3, 4

These are pairing-attributable contractual formula values, not observed paychecks or necessarily additional monthly paycheck cash. United and American amounts are approximate displays because boarding-specific monetary rounding remains unverified. Southwest's outstation-report assumption is conditional, and the reproduced clean agreement retains its source-authentication qualification. Those limitations are part of the result, not footnotes to discard when reusing the table.

What work is being compared?

This is a synthetic mainline Boeing 737-800 benchmark, not an actual awarded pairing or a claim that all four carriers sell these flights. It uses a Los Angeles (LAX) domicile, a Palm Springs (PSP) overnight, 150 passengers on every sector, full prescribed staffing, and an ordinary nonlead/nonpremium cabin position. For this test, the selected seat-configuration inputs are United 166, Alaska 161, Southwest 175 and American 172; these are not a fleet-wide inventory. Installed seats and the 150 passengers carried are different inputs. In particular, 150 passengers must not be substituted for American's installed-seat count when applying its boarding rules. The reviewed positions are United non-Purser/nonlanguage without a qualifying incentive or domestic galley premium, Alaska B-position, Southwest non-A, and American nonlead/nonpremium. These are the preserved common-work assumptions, not findings about typical assignments. 5

All times are Pacific Daylight Time, UTC−07:00. SAN denotes San Diego. Scheduled and actual flight times are identical.

The common work therefore contains eight 55-minute sectors, 7 hours 20 minutes of block time, and three 60-minute turns per duty. The reviewed U.S. Department of Transportation / Bureau of Transportation Statistics (DOT/BTS) distance evidence gives 109 miles for LAX–SAN and 85 miles for SAN–PSP in both directions. Under the reviewed Alaska and Southwest short-sector provisions, each modeled leg produces one native TFP. Southwest's historical mileage-book terminology retains the narrow provenance qualification recorded in the research. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Actual passenger boarding is stipulated from 30 to 10 minutes before departure. The United and American payment calculation nevertheless uses their reviewed 40-minute standard/scheduled boarding unit; it does not substitute the 20 minutes during which passengers physically enter the aircraft. Alaska uses its 0.5-TFP payment per eligible boarding event. All eight boardings satisfy the stated eligibility assumptions; no deadhead, reboarding, cancellation, position premium or other premium event is included. 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7

The surrounding synthetic history contains no other duty, Reserve, standby, training or deadhead from September 22 through October 19 or October 22 through December 31, and no other relevant October/fourth-quarter credits. Lawfully adjusted lineholder status is stipulated. A monthly line consisting of these two duty periods is not asserted to be typical or ordinarily awardable. The overnight hotel room is stipulated available from 16:30 to 07:30, with 15-minute transfers each way. The modeled release-to-report interval is at least 16 hours 5 minutes. These rest and preparation assumptions support a controlled research example, not dispatch approval, a complete line-building assessment, or approval of an individual employee's schedule. Boeing's airport-planning material is used only as physical-plausibility context, not as a carrier-specific task minimum. 5

Which minimum-pay provision wins?

The calculation compares alternatives at their contractual level. It does not add every minimum or rig as a separate payment. A rig is a contractual minimum-pay calculation based on duty time, trip duration or related factors. 1, 2, 3, 4

United. Day one compares 220 flight minutes with 475 ÷ 2 = 237.5 duty-ratio minutes; day two compares 220 with 460 ÷ 2 = 230. Their daily winners total 467.5 credited minutes. The trip-ratio candidate is 1,915 ÷ 3.5 = 547.142857… minutes. The five-hour average minimum across two duties supplies 600 minutes, or ten credited hours, and wins. Eight 40-minute boardings are then paid separately at half rate without increasing those ten core credited hours. 1

10 × $57.26 + 8 × (40 ÷ 60) × ($57.26 ÷ 2) = $725.293333…

The analytical display is approximately $725.29. It does not assert a verified event-level or monthly payroll-rounding rule.

Alaska. Four native TFP per duty and the four-TFP duty-period minimum yield eight TFP across the two duties. The five-TFP average-duty-period guarantee raises the pairing to ten core TFP. Eight eligible boardings add four TFP outside that minimum-pay calculation. This is not a five-TFP floor independently applied to every duty day in every possible pairing. 2

(10 core TFP + 8 × 0.5 boarding TFP) × $49.44 = $692.16

Alaska boarding TFP must not be described as universally excluded from every qualifying-credit measure; the applicable benefit or threshold definition remains important. 2

Southwest. In the conditional 45-minute outstation-report case, each duty compares four earned TFP, a four-TFP daily minimum, and 0.74 TFP per duty hour. The duty-ratio calculations are 0.74 × (490 ÷ 60) = 6.043333… and 0.74 × (475 ÷ 60) = 5.858333…. Rounded to two decimal places under the reviewed rule, the daily winners are 6.04 and 5.86 TFP, totaling 11.90. The trip-hour-ratio calculation is (1,930 ÷ 60) ÷ 3 = 10.722222…, rounded to 10.72 TFP. The 6.5-TFP average-daily guarantee across two original domicile days supplies 13 TFP, and wins. The reviewed version/scenario provides no separate ordinary boarding-event addition for this defined employee. 3

The official annotated provisions and post-ratification pay chart corroborate the specified calculation. They do not authenticate the complete bytes of the retained clean reproduction. 3

American. Each duty compares 220 block minutes, 475 ÷ 2 = 237.5 duty-ratio minutes, and the applicable 180-minute daily floor. The daily winners total 475 minutes. The trip-ratio candidate is 547.142857… minutes. The five-hour average minimum across two duties supplies 600 minutes, or ten credited hours, and wins. Boarding is paid separately without adding core credit. 4

Identical flights need not mean identical duty burdens

Reporting and release rules change the modeled employee's duty burden. Duty time is the sum of both report-to-release periods; it excludes the off-duty overnight interval. Time away from base runs from the first report to the final release and includes that overnight interval. It is not time continuously on duty.

Each ratio below divides the unrounded pairing formula value by the named denominator in hours, then rounds the descriptive result to two decimals. It does not convert the contractual payroll rate into a new hourly wage. Significance

For this work, United and American select the same ten core hours and the same eight 40-minute boarding quantities. Their approximately $5.83 difference at 5.5 years is therefore explained by the dated base rates used in the formulas. Alaska's result comes from ten core TFP plus four boarding TFP; Southwest's comes from thirteen core TFP without an ordinary separate boarding addition under the reviewed provisions. These are different contractual mechanisms, not evidence that hourly compensation inherently beats TFP. Nor should Alaska and Southwest's different TFP systems be treated as interchangeable merely because their units share a name. 1, 2, 3, 4

This example demonstrates contractual value for defined work. It does not compare every component of contract strength. It does not establish progress from prior agreements or representative performance in practice. It supplies no annual-earnings estimate, whole-contract winner, union ranking or newly adopted score.

Reimbursements, per diem, employer retirement contributions, employee-dependent matching, pension value, profit sharing and one-time payments are excluded from the wage subtotal rather than treated as having no value. Monthly guarantees and other monthly adjustments may affect whether pairing value becomes additional paycheck cash. 5

Remaining qualifications

United and American monetary rounding. The exact formulas can be reproduced, but authoritative boarding-specific monetary-rounding stages have not been established. United's reviewed senior 40-minute chart entry of $29.15 also remains unreconciled with $87.47 ÷ 3 = $29.156666…. That is an unresolved source/processing question, not a finding of underpayment. The reviewed United portal article identifies a communication channel, not a rounding rule. American's public event-tracking guidance and login-gated calculator do not supply the missing processing specification. 5, 6, 8

Southwest reporting and source identity. The 45-minute reporting model remains conditional pending the applicable October notice/table. The reproduced clean agreement remains qualified at the complete issuer-byte level while the official annotated provisions and post-ratification pay chart supply positive corroboration. The unresolved qualifications are not proof that the relevant notice or clean source does not exist. 3, 5

Sources and reproduction

The work record, all three cohorts' rates, minimum-pay candidates, boarding quantities and denominators above provide the inputs needed to reproduce the displayed results. Arithmetic was separately recomputed during technical review and remained unchanged after the fixed-scenario calculator's input safeguards were corrected. Those checks validate the calculation for these stated inputs, not arbitrary schedules, actual pay statements, or independent human contract adjudication. New work patterns require a new applicability review.

Contract text and dated implementation materials supply the rules. CrewSignal supplies the synthetic facts and calculations; they are not an additional independent source for contractual entitlements. The evidence cutoff is October 28, 2026 Pacific, while the work dates remain October 20–21. Later retrieval does not import a later pay rate into the example. The next review triggers are authoritative rounding guidance, the applicable Southwest reporting notice/table, a better-authenticated Southwest final text, or a source change affecting these calculations.
