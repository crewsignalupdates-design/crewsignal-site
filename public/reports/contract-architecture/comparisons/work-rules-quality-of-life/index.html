How to interpret these scores

Numerical scorecards are summaries of contract design, not measurements of union diligence, actual grievance recovery, or value per dollar of dues. A defensible substantive comparison checks the operative contract and amendments, identifies matching employee circumstances, and compares both entitlements and exceptions. Published scores are useful starting points, but small differences such as 0.1 should not be treated as independently validated levels of precision.
