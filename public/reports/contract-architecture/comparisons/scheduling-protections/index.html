Scheduling Protections

How effectively does the agreement limit schedule construction, involuntary assignments, and disruption?

What matters: Endeavor uses preferential bidding, minimum days off, and defined limits on extensions and junior assignments, with specified exceptions. LOA 20 adds a payment floor for qualifying five-day line-built trips; later LOA 24 provides a separate conditional failed-commute reporting pathway.

Endeavor Air: what the provisions establish

Contract score: 4.3/5. This value is the earlier published assessment of the base agreement and LOA 20. Subsequent amendments discussed here have not been separately reflected in a recalculated score. See CrewSignal's Endeavor scorecard. The contract provisions below explain its strengths and limitations; they do not constitute a new numerical calculation.

IssueProtection or procedureQualificationSource

Full-time schedules generally carry 10 to 12 minimum days off depending on bid-month length; voluntary waivers and proration are specified.The line-building system remains subject to contractual parameters and a union review process.CBA §5.C (PDF p.30); CBA §5.D.3 (PDF p.31)
Involuntary assignmentsSection 5.H restricts extensions and junior assignments, including numerical limits, decline rights and premium-pay terms.The last-available exception and applicable premiums mean the rules are bounded, not an absolute veto.CBA §5.H.5–6 (PDF pp.41–42)
Five-day tripsLOA 20 §C provides at least 25 hours of Pay Credit for qualifying five-day trips built in line construction.The additional value is for pay only, not schedule credit; it does not replace duty/rest or monthly scheduling requirements.LOA 20 §C (PDF p.2)
Failed-commute noticeLOA 24 revises Section 6.L for qualifying failed commutes, including reporting and travel-option requirements.Protection against discipline is conditional and does not turn missed duty into automatic pay or schedule credit.LOA 24 §6.L (PDF p.2)

Important limitation: A 25-in-5 payment floor is not additional schedule credit. LOA 24's commuter protection applies only when its specific eligibility, notice, and documentation requirements are met.

How to interpret these scores

Numerical scorecards are summaries of contract design, not measurements of union diligence, actual grievance recovery, or value per dollar of dues. A defensible substantive comparison checks the operative contract and amendments, identifies matching employee circumstances, and compares both entitlements and exceptions. Published scores are useful starting points, but small differences such as 0.1 should not be treated as independently validated levels of precision.

Read the CrewSignal Contract Analysis Criteria for the structural methodology. The separate Reserve-specific evaluation uses a different framework.
