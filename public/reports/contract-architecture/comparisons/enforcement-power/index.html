Contract Architecture & Comparative Analysis

Enforcement Power

Can a flight attendant challenge a violation and obtain a binding decision within a defined process?

What matters: Investigations, grievances, neutral System Board arbitration, and mediation give employees defined enforcement procedures. Section 1 provides expedited arbitration for scope disputes. Revised LOA 22 also establishes a distinct safety-reporting process; neither route establishes a universal cash penalty for every contract violation.

Contract score: 4.0/5. This value is the earlier published assessment of the base agreement and LOA 20. Subsequent amendments discussed here have not been separately reflected in a recalculated score. See CrewSignal's Endeavor scorecard. The contract provisions below explain its strengths and limitations; they do not constitute a new numerical calculation.

How to interpret these scores

Numerical scorecards are summaries of contract design, not measurements of union diligence, actual grievance recovery, or value per dollar of dues. A defensible substantive comparison checks the operative contract and amendments, identifies matching employee circumstances, and compares both entitlements and exceptions. Published scores are useful starting points, but small differences such as 0.1 should not be treated as independently validated levels of precision.

Read the CrewSignal Contract Analysis Criteria for the structural methodology. The separate Reserve-specific evaluation uses a different framework.
