Pay & Credit Quality

What guarantees and pay-credit rules compensate flight attendants for assigned work, reassignment, and trip structure?

What matters: Endeavor combines a 75-hour full-time monthly guarantee with trip and minimum-day pay protections. LOA 20 revises hourly rates and per diem and adds a pay-only floor for qualifying five-day trips. Later LOA 24 distinguishes protection against discipline for certain failed commutes from the pay consequences of missed assignments.

Endeavor Air: what the provisions establish

Contract score: 4.2/5. This value is the earlier published assessment of the base agreement and LOA 20. Subsequent amendments discussed here have not been separately reflected in a recalculated score. See CrewSignal's Endeavor scorecard. The contract provisions below explain its strengths and limitations; they do not constitute a new numerical calculation.

IssueProtection or procedureQualificationSource
Monthly minimumSection 18.D establishes a 75-hour full-time monthly guarantee and identifies when it is prorated, reduced, or supplemented by above-guarantee work.The guarantee is not unconditional for periods of non-active service, drops, missed assignments, or other specified events.CBA §18.D (PDF p.105)
Trip and minimum-day paySection 18.E protects qualifying originally scheduled trip value. Section 18.F supplies minimum-day pay with distinctions among trips and partial trip additions.Exceptions and separate treatment of pay credit versus schedule credit affect the actual value.CBA §18.E (PDF p.106); CBA §18.F (PDF p.107)
Extension pay changesLOA 20 replaces the Section 18 wage table through April 2027, updates per diem, and provides 25 hours of pay-only credit for qualifying five-day line-built trips.Rates take effect on their stated dates; geographic-stipend payments under §18.N remain optional and base-specific.LOA 20 §A (PDF p.1); LOA 20 §§B–C (PDF p.2); LOA 20 §D (PDF p.3)
Pay following a qualifying missed commuteLOA 24 adds conditions under which an eligible flight attendant may avoid discipline after a failed commute.Under the letter, missed flying or Reserve availability generally reduces the guarantee unless its specified return-to-duty conditions apply. A discipline safeguard is not an automatic paid absence.LOA 24 §6.L.4 (PDF p.2); §6.L.6 (PDF p.3)

Important limitation: Pay credit and schedule credit are distinct. The 25-in-5 floor applies only to qualifying line-built trips, and LOA 24 does not create an unconditional paid absence for commuting disruptions.

American Airlines: Guarantees and additional compensation

Existing APFA score: 4.5/5. The 2024–2029 agreement establishes the provisions summarized below. Return to American's scorecard. The score is retained from the existing assessment, not recalculated from these three examples.

ProvisionWhat is protectedCondition or exceptionContract source
Monthly guaranteesSection 3.B provides 71-hour lineholder and 75-hour Reserve minimums, with stated circumstances that reduce or prorate them.The two guarantees apply to different work statuses and cannot be compared as universally earned monthly hours.CBA §3.B
Boarding paySection 3.D pays half the hourly rate for qualifying scheduled boarding minutes.This is pay without credit and includes standby and other exceptions.CBA §3.D
Duty, trip, and sit rigsSection 11.D provides a 1-for-3.5 trip rig and 1-for-2 duty rig, both involving pay and credit, plus a sit rig over 2:30 that provides pay without credit.Each rig has its own calculation and exclusions; the sit rig's implementation timetable must be checked.CBA §11.D; CBA §11.D.6

Reading the result: This score recognizes strong contractual pay mechanisms. Boarding and sit payments cannot be treated as credited flight time, and a higher pay rate alone does not establish a superior total duty-day result.

Some contractual provisions were phased in, and APFA publishes later agreements separately. For current operational applicability, consult the APFA electronic contract library and implementation information.

How to interpret these scores

Numerical scorecards are summaries of contract design, not measurements of union diligence, actual grievance recovery, or value per dollar of dues. A defensible substantive comparison checks the operative contract and amendments, identifies matching employee circumstances, and compares both entitlements and exceptions. Published scores are useful starting points, but small differences such as 0.1 should not be treated as independently validated levels of precision.

Read the CrewSignal Contract Analysis Criteria for the structural methodology. The separate Reserve-specific evaluation uses a different framework.
