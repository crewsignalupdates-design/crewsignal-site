Pay & Credit Quality

What guarantees and pay-credit rules compensate flight attendants for assigned work, reassignment, and trip structure?

What matters: Endeavor combines a 75-hour full-time monthly guarantee with trip guarantees and minimum-day pay provisions. LOA 20 revises hourly rates and per diem and adds a 25-hour pay-credit floor for certain five-day trips. These pay protections depend on their specific triggering conditions.

How to interpret these scores

Numerical scorecards are summaries of contract design, not measurements of union diligence, actual grievance recovery, or value per dollar of dues. A defensible substantive comparison checks the operative contract and amendments, identifies matching employee circumstances, and compares both entitlements and exceptions. Published scores are useful starting points, but small differences such as 0.1 should not be treated as independently validated levels of precision.

Read the CrewSignal Contract Analysis Criteria for the structural methodology. The separate Reserve-specific evaluation uses a different framework.
