Agreement Metadata

Hawaiian's 2020 flight attendant collective bargaining agreement remains the base document, supplemented by the 2025 Comprehensive Extension ratified April 17, 2025. The extension carries the amendable date through February 2028 while joint Alaska/Hawaiian contract bargaining continues.

Contractual rights must be read across the base CBA and the extension. CrewSignal separately preserves the base-to-extension score changes.

Contract Architecture Overview

The agreement remains a comprehensive long-haul and Hawaii-specific scheduling, Reserve, pay and dispute-resolution system. The 2025 extension adds targeted economic and operational improvements but does not replace the underlying architecture or eliminate its multiple conditions and cross-references.

The updated contract score is 23.3/30, reproduced from CrewSignal's existing extension analysis. The original 22.5/30 assessment remains the historical 2020 baseline.

Scheduling & Assignment Framework

Preferential bidding, Reserve assignments, rest and open-time provisions continue to control operations. The 2025 extension links certain Reserve Guaranteed Day Off pickups to actual rest and adds open-flying status information that can improve visibility.

The new status indicators do not themselves guarantee that flying is awarded. Employee qualifications and rest requirements still govern eligibility. Base scheduling provisions; 2025 scheduling amendments.

Economic Structure

The extension establishes a 6% pay raise followed by two scheduled 3% increases, replaces Hawaiian's former profit-sharing framework with participation in the Alaska Air Group Performance Based Pay program, and adjusts related economic terms. The PBP's stated 5% of eligible earnings is a target, not an unconditional guaranteed payment.

Other pay and credit rules continue under the base CBA except where amended. 2025 extension economics; economic score adjustment.

Enforcement & Dispute Resolution Architecture

The base agreement retains its grievance and System Board structure. The extension modifies selected panel and meeting procedures without creating a new universal automatic damages remedy for contract breaches.

Company performance, awards and payment to individual flight attendants remain separate evidence questions. Base grievance provisions; extension modifications.

Structural Strengths, Weaknesses & Comparative Flags

Strengths: A ratified multi-year extension provides scheduled wage improvements, modified profit-sharing participation, targeted Reserve and open-flying transparency, and certain additional paid-time-off and safety-related provisions while joint bargaining proceeds.

Limitations: The larger CBA remains complex, the extension does not significantly change retained Company discretion, and some benefits depend on implementation or eligibility. The 2025 extension improves the previously published structural profile from 22.5/30 to 23.3/30, not through a new CrewSignal calculation. Read the scored extension comparison.
