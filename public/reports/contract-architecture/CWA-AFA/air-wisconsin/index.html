Agreement Metadata

At a glance: Air Wisconsin's agreement protects eleven monthly days off, limits annual drafting, provides a 70-hour full-month guarantee, and makes Company failure to meet certain grievance deadlines a basis to grant the grievance. Some rights require a timely employee request. See the scorecard.

Section 28 states an automatic-renewal and Section 6 notice process. The agreement became amendable October 1, 2022; CWA-AFA announced on September 14, 2026 that a proposed successor contract did not ratify. Duration clause; Vote result.

Following ownership change and recalls, CWA-AFA announced an immediate wage increase in January 2026. The published 2020 wage schedule should not be treated as a verified present-day pay table without the later executed amendment. Union announcement.

Contract Architecture Overview

This agreement groups scope, definitions, management rights and grievance procedures before compensation, hours, scheduling, leave and working conditions. Related pay and scheduling questions still require multiple sections and appended letters. Table of contents.

Section 3 broadly reserves management authority but expressly subordinates it to the agreement. A specific negotiated limit therefore remains significant even where management has broad residual discretion. Section 3.

Scheduling & Assignment Framework

Section 11 sets bid and award windows, caps individual bid lines at 95 block hours and base-average bid-line construction at 88 block hours. Ready Reserve is limited to 25% of Reserve positions at a domicile and normally uses nine-hour shifts. Bid-line rules; Ready Reserve.

Drafting on days off is capped at ten assignments per calendar year with defined seniority order and pay or day-off choices. Section 10 separately caps actual monthly block flying at 95 hours, but allows voluntary waiver. Drafting; Hours cap.

Economic Structure

The preserved agreement establishes a 70-hour monthly guarantee for fully available lineholders and Reserves; trip and daily-pay guarantees contain exceptions. Deadhead receives 50% of the applicable flight rate, and qualifying lineholder days have a 3.5-hour minimum. Monthly and trip guarantees; Deadhead and daily pay.

Duty and trip rigs compare one hour for every 2.25 duty hours, one hour for every four hours away from base, and actual or scheduled flight time. Drafting or qualifying resequencing may earn 1.5 times the hourly rate, subject to a no-pyramiding rule. Rigs and premiums; No pyramiding.

Rate qualification: The historical rate table does not incorporate the separately announced 2026 wage adjustment, whose full terms remain unverified here.

Enforcement & Dispute Resolution Architecture

Section 4 provides discipline and contract grievance procedures; non-disciplinary grievances generally must be filed within 30 days after awareness. Section 5 establishes a three-person System Board with a neutral and final, binding decisions in properly referred cases. Section 4.C; Section 5.

Specific consequence: Section 4.F.1 says Company noncompliance with grievance-procedure time limits results in granting the grievance, while missed deadlines by the union or grievant result in denial; deadlines can be extended by agreement. This is not the same as automatic advancement to arbitration. Section 4.F.1.

A granted grievance does not independently verify a particular monetary recovery. Mediation requires mutual agreement, and actual employee payments and compliance are separate factual questions. Grievance mediation.

Structural Strengths, Weaknesses & Comparative Flags

Strengths: Eleven guaranteed monthly days off with specified corrections, a ten-per-year drafting limit, Ready Reserve restrictions, defined pay rigs and a direct consequence for missed Company grievance deadlines. Days-off protection; Drafting; Grievance rules.

Limitations: Rights may depend on timely requests and classification; management retains broad authority, and the 95-hour actual-block flying limit can restrict additional flight opportunities unless voluntarily waived. The economic and quality-of-life effects of that limitation depend on actual flying opportunities and individual circumstances. Management rights; Voluntary hours waiver.

Successor bargaining remained unresolved after the September 2026 ratification rejection. Later wage changes must not be silently added to historic contractual ratings.
