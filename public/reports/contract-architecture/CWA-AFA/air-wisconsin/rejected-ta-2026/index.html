Air Wisconsin: What the rejected 2026 proposal would have changed

Contract Architecture & Comparative Analysis
Rejected September 14, 2026 · Not an operative agreement

The principal findings

The union's rejected-TA summary describes improvements to deadhead pay, Reserve shifts, bid-line days-off repairs and commuter lodging. It also preserves significant Company choices over home basing, supplemental flying and certain duty assignments. None of these proposed changes became effective merely because they appeared in a rejected settlement.

Source limit: This comparison is based on CWA-AFA's 20-page August 28, 2026 summary, not a complete authenticated tentative agreement. It describes potential directions of change without assigning hypothetical numerical ratings.

Key Documents

  • Earlier signed CWA-AFA agreement
  • Rejected 2026 TA summary (CWA-AFA, 20 pages)

The union reported six votes in favor, but did not disclose a complete vote tally or document members' individual reasons for voting against the proposal.

Earlier negotiated gains are not new TA gains

The summary separately identifies a January 19, 2026 23% wage increase and a May 21, 2026 LOA covering scheduled increases, per diem and a 70-to-72-hour minimum monthly guarantee change. These preceded the September vote and must not be added again as benefits attributable to the rejected proposal. See the union's summary of those earlier agreements.

What remains unverified

The complete rejected agreement, incorporation language, implementation schedule and final clause-level redlines have not been authenticated in this review. The summary cannot establish every unchanged or conceded provision. A reproducible six-domain point-allocation method is also necessary before publishing a numerical hypothetical score.

A later ratified successor must be examined separately against the operative agreement, these already negotiated interim changes and the rejected proposal.
