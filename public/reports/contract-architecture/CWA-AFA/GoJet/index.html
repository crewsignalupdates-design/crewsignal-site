Agreement Metadata

At a glance: GoJet's successor provides a seventy-five-hour guarantee, defined minimum-day and deadhead pay, and conditional later boarding-pay and Reserve improvements. View the six-domain findings.

GoJet flight attendants ratified the 2025 successor agreement on August 14, 2025. Section 31 states an effective date of August 16, 2025 and a term through August 16, 2029, with a renewal and notice provision. The PDF retains its pre-ratification TA cover and shows a DocuSign envelope identifier.

Some economic improvements and Reserve reforms have separate implementation terms. The existence of a contractual clause does not, by itself, establish when a conditional benefit appeared on payroll. Read Section 31.

Contract Architecture Overview

The successor agreement uses 31 numbered sections and incorporated letters. Section 1 protects bargaining-unit flying and establishes an expedited scope arbitration route; Section 3 retains substantial management rights except as expressly restricted.

The negotiated design distinguishes lineholder trip protection, Reserve duties, minimum pay, and grievance enforcement. Several benefits depend on implementation milestones or classification as a lineholder rather than Reserve. Section 1; Section 3.

Scheduling & Assignment Framework

Section 7 uses PBS and generally guarantees eleven scheduled days off in 28–30-day bid periods or twelve in 31–32-day bid periods, subject to availability, proration and employee waiver. Section 7.B limits ordinary trips to five days/four nights. Recorded SOC scheduling calls and defined assignment rules improve auditability.

Section 8.A initially defines a Reserve day as 0001–2400. It commits the parties to negotiate and implement a fourteen-hour Reserve Availability Period with limited flexing within two years of the agreement's effective date. That future commitment is not proof the fourteen-hour system was already implemented. Section 7; Section 8.

Economic Structure

Section 4.F provides a 75-hour minimum guarantee, with reductions for dropped or lower-value assignments and specified unpaid absences. Section 4.C establishes a qualifying four-hour minimum day, but excludes certain partial trips, Reserve cancellations and unpaid absences. Section 9.E provides 100% pay and credit for qualifying deadheads.

Section 4.G provides boarding pay at one-half the hourly rate, above guarantee and without virtual bid credit, but its stated March 1, 2026 implementation date was conditional on specified ratifications at United and another regional carrier. A later implementation confirmation is needed to describe the provision as actually in payroll operation. Section 4; Section 9.

Enforcement & Dispute Resolution Architecture

Section 24 provides just-cause discipline, representation and specified grievance/appeal periods. Under Section 24.E, a Company failure to hold a hearing or issue a decision on time results in a deemed denial and automatic advancement to the next step or System Board—not a grant of the grievance.

Section 25 provides a three-member System Board with a neutral; Section 1.H offers expedited scope arbitration. These procedural protections should not be confused with universal punitive damages or verified wage recovery. Section 24; Section 25.

Structural Strengths, Weaknesses & Comparative Flags

Established protections: A 75-hour guarantee, a conditional four-hour minimum day, 100% qualifying deadhead credit, more explicit day-off and scheduling rules, and a formal grievance/neutral arbitration system.

Important limits: Broad residual management authority, exceptions to minimum-day pay, the conditional boarding-pay trigger, and Reserve availability that initially remained 24 hours pending future RAP implementation. A negotiated future mechanism is not the same as a presently enforceable shorter window.

Context Notes

GoJet's ratified successor agreement is a different bargaining generation from Eastern's first contract. Comparisons should distinguish already-operative rights from future milestones, especially boarding pay and the intended fourteen-hour Reserve RAP. No six-domain numeric rating is published until a reproducible point-based methodology is applied; unscored is not zero.
